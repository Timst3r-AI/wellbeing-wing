# 0046 — Synthetic-Only Public Law and the External Adoption Boundary (DR-W7-01)

**Status:** Accepted by human reviewer, 2026-08-18. Not a build instruction. Authorises no implementation by itself.
**Date:** August 2026 · **Phase:** W7 — First-Contact Governance and Synthetic Model Evaluation · **Deliverable:** **W7-D1** (the **first** of two D1 decision records)
**Position:** the outer fence of the model era, landing **before** DR-W7-02 because boundary-before-contact binds. It decides what public W7 may be made of, what it may never contain, how the lived world outside is referred to, and what every artefact of the coming class must be able to declare about itself. **It creates no artefact of that class, defines no record shape, authors no text, and contacts nothing.**
**Constitutional references:** W0 Laws 1, 3, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13; W0 §10; W0 Non-Goal 7. **No law is amended.**
**North star carried:** generated text may enter the public Wing only as synthetic, governed evaluation evidence — never authority, advice, truth, safety evidence, approval, or a decision about any person.
**Resolves:** none.

---

**Three times this repository has been asked to let real material in through a side door, and three times it has answered the same way: there is no de-identification pathway, because there is no identification to remove. Fixtures were authored from nothing. Development artefacts were authored from nothing. Now a fourth kind of text is coming — text that no one in this repository will have written — and the answer has to hold under a harder case, because this time the material is not merely *about* nothing real, it is produced by something that cannot be asked what it was thinking. This record draws the fence before the field is planted: three lawful origins and no fourth, a closed list of what may never appear, one sentence by which the lived world is named and never elaborated, and a rule that an artefact which cannot say where it came from does not get in.**

## Decision question

**What is public W7 permitted to be made of, what may it never contain, in exactly what words is the external adoption authority referred to, what must every artefact of the coming generated-output class be able to declare about itself, and what ceiling binds text that no human in this repository authored — decided now, as fixed input to DR-W7-02 and to every later W7 deliverable, without creating any artefact of that class?**

## Controlling law

- **The W7-D1 brief (`W7-D1-FCB`), whole** — this record's assigned scope, verbatim: the constructional test, the three lawful origins, the closed exclusion list, the fixed external boundary with its non-naming law, provenance declarability as the admission condition, the non-authority ceiling, and the proof obligations of its §9. **Its §4 architectural ruling on the boundary invariant is carried here unchanged and is not reopened.**
- **The W7 runway (`W7-AR`)** — §4 public/private boundary, §5 synthetic-only law, §6 generated-output artefact law and its nine binding properties, §9 non-goals, §10 tripwires, §11 quality gates, §13 public-safety considerations.
- **W2-D4 (Synthetic Fixture Strategy), whole** — rule 1: *fully synthetic, by construction, never by scrubbing*, with derivation from any real person's health information prohibited including anonymised, aggregated, paraphrased and "inspired by", and **no de-identification pathway because there is no identification to remove**; rule 2's marker-block precedent (§4), the first place in this repository where synthetic provenance was made a mechanically checkable assertion rather than a promise; and §3's format conventions.
- **ADR-0007 (Development Artifact Policy)** — decision 1 (synthetic-only everywhere, always; scrubbing-after a rejected pathway); **decision 2, the headline law: paste is publication** — any fragment entering a document is a new publication event re-checked at paste time, and the original artefact's cleanliness never transfers; decision 4 (no machine-identifying detail — no hostnames, usernames, device names, serial numbers, or local folder paths); decision 6 (no content strings in commit, review, or issue text, **synthetic included**, because synthetic content in messages trains reviewers to stop checking).
- **ADR-0034 A4 decisions 10–12** — every evaluation artefact synthetic-only with no evaluation channel to a person; harness prompts and execution transcripts governed under the W2-D4 discipline and the public-safety scan exactly as documents are; no real grant anywhere in evaluation, and any evaluation involving a real person's content requiring its own separate governed authority that nothing there creates or anticipates. **B6 decisions 32–35** — the evaluation artefact class, its permanent never-become rules, and decision 34's rule that such records are **scanned exactly as documents are, synthetic-only, with no exemption for machine production.** **Decision 37** — no evaluation result presented as a safety, health, or `certif-` claim in any surface, summary, or derived artefact, in this era or any later one.
- **ADR-0033 B1 decisions 22–25** — model output is agent-origin by construction, carries no authority, cannot be relabelled at or after the boundary, is never evidence or ground truth for any test, and never mints authority for any datum, label, record, or person; fluency, confidence, and repetition create nothing.
- **ADR-0039 rule 12** — declarability as the admission condition: what cannot declare itself as data cannot be audited, and what cannot be audited is unlawful. **ADR-0043 decision 13** completes that rule for surfaces under *no declaration, no pass*; this record completes it for provenance.
- **ADR-0038** — the growable-only discipline for closed prohibition lists, and the stem convention that lets a record carry a ban without tripping the scan that enforces it.

## Decisions

### Part A — The door

1. **This record decides the fence and creates nothing inside it.** After its landing the repository contains exactly one new governed artefact: this record. No generated text exists, no specimen is authored, no record of the coming class exists, no schema field is defined, no harness exists, no dependency is added, and no model has been contacted. Every statement below about "a W7 artefact of the class" describes a future thing that only later authorised landings may bring into existence.

2. **This record binds DR-W7-02 and every later W7 deliverable, and is consumed rather than re-decided by them.** DR-W7-02 defines what first contact *is*; this record defines what public W7 may be *made of*. A later record may extend the lists here through its own ceremony; none may narrow them by convenience.

### Part B — Synthetic-only by construction, never by scrubbing

3. **Public W7 is synthetic-only by construction, never by scrubbing.** W2-D4 rule 1 and ADR-0007 decision 1 are carried into the generated-output class unweakened and unqualified. Material is authored from nothing. Derivation from any real person's content — including anonymised, aggregated, paraphrased, summarised, statistically derived, or "inspired by" — is prohibited. **There is no de-identification pathway in W7, because there is no identification to remove.** Scrubbing has now been rejected as a pathway three times in this corpus — for fixtures, for development artefacts, and here — and the reason is the same each time: scrubbing requires judgement at exactly the moment judgement fails.

4. **The test is constructional, not custodial.** A W7 artefact, path, field, or process that *could* carry real-person content is unlawful **regardless of whether it currently does.** Emptiness is not conformance. A field that would accept a real transcript is a defect on the day it is defined, not on the day it is filled. This is the difference between a fence and a habit, and W7 is required to have a fence.

5. **Paste is publication, carried into the new class as capture is publication.** ADR-0007 decision 2 binds every act that moves text into a W7 record: **the capture is a new publication event, checked at capture time by the act that captures it.** Upstream cleanliness never transfers — not from a model, not from a specimen, not from an earlier record, not from a fixture. **A capture that cannot be checked at capture time may not be made.**

6. **Synthetic content stays out of the repository's own commentary.** ADR-0007 decision 6 binds W7 unchanged: no generated text, specimen text, capture excerpt, or probe wording appears in a commit message, a board row, a README, a registry role, a publication packet, or any review text. **Identifiers, categories, counts, and behaviours only.** The reason is the one ADR-0007 gives and this record does not improve on: content strings in messages train reviewers to stop checking.

### Part C — The three lawful input origins

7. **Every input to every W7 artefact traces to exactly one of three origins, and the set is closed:**
   - **(a) Authored synthetic material** — written inside this repository, for this purpose, from nothing, under W2-D4 rule 1.
   - **(b) Existing repository fixtures and fixture-derived material** — governed by W2-D4 whole, including its marker, identifier, and ontology rules.
   - **(c) Governed public repository records** — the accepted corpus itself, cited rather than reproduced.

8. **A fourth origin is unlawful.** Not a finding to be dispositioned, not an exception to be bounded — unlawful. An artefact whose input cannot be placed in exactly one of (a), (b), or (c) does not land.

9. **No-recirculation: capture is terminal.** Generated output and specimen text may be **captured** into a record of the coming class. Captured text may **never** become an input to anything else — not a prompt, not a probe, not a fixture, not a catalogue string, not a doctrine record, not a summary, not another capture, and not origin (a) by later re-labelling. **The laundering path this closes is the obvious one:** text that could not enter as authority walks back in as material, wearing the origin of whoever moved it.

10. **The set is growable only by governed record**, following ADR-0038's discipline for closed lists: a fourth origin, if one is ever needed, is a decision record with its own ceremony and its own proof — never a convenience at a landing.

### Part D — The closed exclusion list

11. **No public W7 record, artefact, path, field, filename, or commentary may contain:** real-person data of any kind · any identified person's room, health, movement, food, device, contemplative, or journal material · real wearable or device data · private relationship or lived-interaction material · private model transcripts · credentials, tokens, keys, secrets, or private configuration · **machine-identifying detail beyond OS class — hostnames, usernames, device names, serial numbers, or local folder paths** (ADR-0007 decision 4; repository-relative paths are not machine-identifying and are unaffected) · a model binary · a real-person evaluation channel · private adoption implementation detail · or anything that would turn the public Wing into a live personal instrument.

12. **The list is growable only, never narrowable.** It may be extended by any governed record that finds a gap. It may not be shortened, softened, or read down by a landing, a milestone, a schedule, or a finding that would be inconvenient to honour. **A W7 landing that would be easier if an item were absent is the exact circumstance the list exists for.**

13. **No exemption for machine production.** ADR-0034 decision 34 binds: artefacts of the coming class are scanned **exactly as documents are**, and the fact that a machine produced the bytes buys nothing. **The consequence, stated here and mechanised by W7-D2:** because generated prose cannot be enumerated in advance, a scan finding inside it is a **governed event with a disposition path** — the record does not land, or it lands with the finding recorded and the text bounded or excluded. **It is never silently allowlisted.** This record binds the principle and forbids the shortcut; the mechanism is W7-D2's and must be designed inside this rule rather than around it.

### Part E — The external-boundary invariant and the non-naming law

14. **The fixed invariant is the clause:**

> **a separate governed authority outside this repository**

15. **Sentence-position grammar is permitted, and nothing else is.** Permitted: a leading capital at sentence start; lowercase mid-sentence; relative-clause linkage where grammar requires it. **Not permitted:** synonym, paraphrase, expansion, location, date, participant, practice, project name, plan name, implementation detail, or explanatory footnote. **The invariant is the words; the grammar around them may bend only as far as a sentence requires and not one word further.**

16. **The non-naming law.** No public record may name, describe, locate, date, specify, implement, schedule, or depend upon that external authority; may not enumerate its rooms, devices, participants, or practices; may not state that it exists as a project, a plan, or a phase; and may not elaborate the invariant clause by synonym, paraphrase, expansion, or footnote.

17. **Stating the clause creates nothing.** It opens no phase, schedules no work, implies no timeline, confers no authority, and establishes no dependency. **No W7 artefact may depend on that authority, wait for it, prepare for it, or be shaped by an assumption about it.** ADR-0034 A4 decision 12 already holds that evaluation involving a real person's content would require its own separate governed authority, *which nothing there creates or anticipates* — and nothing here creates or anticipates it either.

18. **No erratum is required for `W7-AR` §14.** Its wording — *"any real-person adoption, which remains a separate governed authority outside this repository"* — is a grammatical variant permitted by decision 15, not a doctrinal inconsistency, because it preserves the invariant clause unchanged. **This is stated in the record so that the first mechanical check cannot be mistaken for a finding against the runway.**

### Part F — Provenance declarability as the admission condition

19. **ADR-0039 rule 12, completed for provenance.** A W7 artefact that cannot declare, **as data**, where its inputs came from **cannot land.** What cannot declare itself cannot be audited, and what cannot be audited is unlawful. ADR-0043 decision 13 completed this rule for surfaces under *no declaration, no pass*; this record completes it for origin.

20. **The minimum declarable facts**, named as a required capability and not as a schema: for each artefact of the coming class — that it is synthetic, as a literal machine-checkable assertion; the origin of each input, drawn from the closed three-value set of decision 7; the citation or identifier locating that input; whether the artefact holds captured text and, if so, that the capture is terminal under decision 9; and the exclusion-list and scan status of its own bytes. **The precedent for the shape is W2-D4 §4's marker block** — the first place this repository made synthetic provenance a mechanically checkable assertion rather than a promise, with `synthetic` literally `true` as an assertion target and a fixed verbatim notice.

21. **The field shape is not decided here.** Field names, key order, nesting, and the record schema are **W7-D2's**, wholly, within decisions 19 and 20. Stating that assignment explicitly is deliberate: silence would read as a decision, and it is not one.

22. **Undeclarable is unlawful, not merely unproven.** An artefact shape that cannot carry these declarations fails this record before it fails any later one — exactly as ADR-0044 Part E held for the catalogue.

### Part G — The non-authority ceiling

23. **Every W7 record that holds generated or specimen text carries this sentence, byte-identically, inside itself:**

> **Generated output is evaluation evidence only. It is not truth, advice, diagnosis, therapy, safety evidence, correctness evidence, clinical validity, legal conformance, production readiness, approval, or a decision about any person.**

24. **Inside the record, never beside it** — the catalogue precedent, where the ceiling sentence is carried byte-identically in every entry so that no excerpt can escape it. A ceiling that lives in a covering document is a ceiling that survives exactly until someone quotes a paragraph.

25. **Specimen parity.** The ceiling binds on **authored synthetic specimens identically to generated output.** Otherwise the option that avoids model contact would produce text under a weaker ceiling than the text it stands in for, and the governed handling would be proven against an easier case than the one it exists for.

26. **The standing model-output law is carried, not restated as new.** ADR-0033 decisions 22–25 bind unchanged: agent-origin by construction, no authority, no relabelling at or after the boundary, never evidence, never ground truth for any test, and no minting of authority for any datum, label, record, or person — with fluency, confidence, and repetition creating nothing. ADR-0034 decision 37 binds unchanged: no result is presented as a safety, health, or `certif-` claim in any surface, summary, board row, or derived artefact, in this era or any later one.

### Part H — Proof obligations

27. **Six proof obligations are specified; each names what fails it, and each declares honestly whether a machine can decide it.** Five are mechanical. **One — P2 — is review-only in full**, for the reasons decision 29 records.

| # | Obligation | Fails when | Decidability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | **Boundary-invariant exact form.** Every occurrence of the external-boundary clause in any tracked record matches decision 14 byte-for-byte, varying only by decision 15's permitted sentence positions. | Any synonym, paraphrase, or inserted or removed word inside the clause. **The check itself fails if the permitted variation set widens.** | **Mechanical.** Scope is the clause and only the clause; adjacent elaboration is P2's subject and is not decidable here |
| **P2** | **Non-naming.** No public record names, describes, locates, dates, specifies, or elaborates the external authority, in any form, whether adjacent to the invariant clause or anywhere else. | Any naming or elaborating material appears, in any wording. | **Review-only, in full** — not partially mechanical, not mechanical for an enumerable subset. Decision 29 states why, and the reason is structural rather than a gap in tooling |
| **P3** | **Origin declaration.** Every artefact of the coming class declares an origin for each input, drawn from the closed three-value set. | A missing declaration, an empty declaration, or a fourth value. | Mechanical |
| **P4** | **Exclusion-list conformance.** The decision 11 list expressed in scannable terms, with the two scan-sensitive families carried as stems so the prohibition stays clean. | Any listed item present in tracked bytes. | Mechanical |
| **P5** | **Ceiling verbatim.** The decision 23 sentence appears byte-identically inside every record of the class that holds generated or specimen text. | Absent, reworded, abbreviated, or moved outside the record. | Mechanical |
| **P6** | **No-recirculation.** No artefact declares an input whose declared origin is a capture. | Any input declaration referencing captured text. | Mechanical |

28. **Implementation timing, stated so it is not discovered later.** **P1 is implemented at this record's own landing**, same-commit-coupled, at `tests/test_w7_boundary_invariant.py` — its subject matter already existed in the published corpus, since `W7-AR`, `W7-D1-FCB`, the phase board and this record all carry the invariant clause, so it was written and proven green against real bytes rather than promised. Because the P1 proof module necessarily contains the invariant tail and negative-control near-misses, the instrument excludes only itself from its scan scope through `SELF_EXCLUDED = frozenset({"tests/test_w7_boundary_invariant.py"})`; the exclusion is asserted to be exactly that one path and to resolve to the module itself, so it cannot widen silently. **Its scope is the clause and only the clause**, and its permitted-variation set is a closed literal guarded by an assertion that fails if the set widens. **P2 is never implementable**, for the structural reasons in decision 29; it is discharged by human review at every landing and by nothing else, and **P1's green result is not evidence for it in any degree.** **P3 through P6 cannot be implemented yet**: they check artefacts of a class that does not exist, and a proof over an empty set is a proof of nothing. They are **specified here and implemented by the landing that first creates an artefact of the class**, following the ADR-0039 decision 10 and ADR-0042 Part F pattern — conformance proofs assigned to the artefacts that first bear the thing being checked. **A specified-but-unimplemented obligation is a debt, not a receipt** — and a permanently review-only obligation is a standing duty, not a debt that some future landing discharges.

29. **Why P2 cannot be mechanical, recorded as doctrine rather than as an apology.** Two independent reasons, either of which alone would be sufficient.

   **(a) A mechanical check for a name that must never be written cannot exist.** A machine check needs a search term. The material P2 forbids is a name that decision 16 bars from appearing anywhere in this repository. **Writing the search term into a test file would put the barred name into the repository and breach the very law the check enforces.** There is no lawful needle, so there is no lawful check. This is not a limitation of the tooling available; it is a property of any prohibition whose subject may not be written down, and it will be equally true of every tool this repository ever adopts.

   **(b) Rule-explaining prose and authority-elaborating prose cannot be told apart without semantic judgement.** A record that *governs* the invariant must quote it, must quote the runway's permitted variant to rule it conformant (decision 18), and must enumerate the barred categories in order to forbid them (decisions 15–16). Legitimate continuations of the clause across the published corpus include *"…, named in public records in exactly one fixed form"*, *"…, with sentence-position grammar permitted"*, and *"…, and no W7 record names, describes, implements, or depends on it"* — each stating the **rule**, none elaborating the **authority**. **Any pattern permissive enough to admit those is permissive enough to admit a real elaboration**, and any pattern strict enough to exclude a real elaboration would fail this record and the runway together. Closing that gap would require an allowlist of permitted continuations — which is precisely the silent allowlisting decision 13 forbids elsewhere in this same record, and this record will not do at its own boundary what it bars at the generated-output boundary.

30. **Mechanical and review-only are kept apart, and the ADR-0038 stem convention is not misapplied here.** The DR-W4-06 decidability discipline binds: **P2 is never reported as a mechanical result, a green suite is never evidence that P2 holds, and no review judgement is smuggled into any green check.** The stem convention that lets ADR-0038 carry a barred display list without tripping the scan does not transfer: stems work because the barred words are writable and enumerable, and P2's subject is neither. **P1's greenness says the clause is intact. It says nothing whatever about whether the authority was named, and no landing packet, closure record, or summary may imply otherwise.**

### Part I — Alternatives, assessed

31. **Scrubbing with review (rejected).** Permit real-derived material where a reviewer confirms it is de-identified. Rejected for the third time in this corpus and for the original reason: judgement under deadline is the failure mode, not the control. A constructional rule needs no decision at capture time, and capture time is exactly when judgement fails.

32. **An open origin set with a prohibition list (rejected).** Permit any input not explicitly forbidden. Rejected: it inverts the burden onto the list, and a list cannot anticipate a source that does not exist yet. **Closed sets fail safe; open sets fail quietly.**

33. **The whole sentence as the boundary invariant (assessed, not adopted).** Considered by the W7-D1 brief and settled by its §4 architectural ruling in favour of the clause. Recorded here because the alternative was real and the reasoning should survive: fixing the whole sentence is stricter but would render the published runway's §14 non-conformant over a variation that changes no meaning, buying an erratum and no protection. **The clause is where the meaning lives; the grammar around it carries none.**

34. **Deferring the ceiling to the record shape (rejected).** Let W7-D2 decide whether and where a ceiling appears. Rejected: the ceiling is a property of the text's status, not of the schema that holds it, and a schema authored without a mandatory ceiling would have to be amended to acquire one. **The ceiling is decided before the container so the container is built around it.**

### Part J — Inheritance and boundaries

35. **What this record hands DR-W7-02:** a closed origin set, a closed exclusion list, a fixed boundary invariant with its non-naming law, a declarability admission condition, a mandatory ceiling with specimen parity, and six specified proof obligations. DR-W7-02 defines first contact by provenance inside this fence and does not re-decide any of it.

36. **What this record hands W7-D2:** the declarability capability of decisions 19–20 to give a shape to, the finding-is-an-event principle of decision 13 to mechanise, and P3 through P6 to implement at the landing that first creates an artefact of the class.

37. **What this record does not touch.** The model boundary and the four options remain wholly W7-D3's. ADR-0033's hosted class stays closed and its local class doctrinally constructable only; **naming neither opens either.** E10, Z4, E12/Z5 stay dormant. The pending ledger, the carried findings, the applicability records, and the Tier 3 rows are untouched.

## Consequences

**Made easier:** W7-D2 receives a fence rather than a debate — origins, exclusions, declarability, and the ceiling are fixed inputs, and the schema can be designed against them in one pass. The first artefact of the class arrives with its provenance checkable rather than asserted. And the phase's hardest public-safety question — what happens when a scan finds something inside text nobody wrote — has an answer before the first byte exists.

**Made harder, deliberately:** every capture costs a check at capture time, and no upstream cleanliness discharges it. Nothing generated may be reused, so any pipeline that would have chained outputs must be redesigned or abandoned. And an artefact shape that cannot declare its provenance is dead on arrival, which will occasionally be inconvenient at exactly the moment it is load-bearing.

**Accepted cost, in two kinds.** **One obligation can never be mechanised.** P2 — the non-naming law's enforcement — is review-only in full and permanently so, which means the strongest law in this record is guarded by the weakest instrument the repository has. That is not a defect to be engineered away later; decision 29 shows why no engineering reaches it. **Four more are specified without being implemented**, and P3–P6 stay visible debts until the artefact class exists. This record pretends otherwise nowhere: **P3–P6 must not be reported as satisfied by anything short of a green check over real artefacts, and P2 must never be reported as satisfied by a green check at all.**

## Constitutional check

- **Law 1** — nothing contacts anyone, nothing runs in the background, and no notification surface exists or is implied.
- **Law 3** — nothing self-promotes: captured text mints no authority, a green provenance check confirms origin and nothing else, and fluency remains nothing.
- **Law 6** — the clinical line holds: no specimen, capture, or record is a diagnosis or a clinical claim, and the ceiling says so inside every record that could be mistaken for one.
- **Law 8** — inference stays closed: nothing here creates a dataset, and no-recirculation forecloses the accumulation path by which one would form.
- **Law 9** — the Meditation wall is untouched: no material of any kind crosses it, and no W7 artefact bridges anything.
- **Law 10** — consent is untouched: W7 has no user, no real grant exists anywhere in it, and no synthetic shape functions as one.
- **Law 11** — no person is gated: nothing here conditions any person's access to anything.
- **Law 13** — every capture is a governed, auditable event with a declared origin, and decision 13 keeps a finding an event rather than a silence.
- **W0 §10** — the agent-to-agent rule is carried whole: text arriving from a model is unverified input everywhere, including inside a record that preserves it faithfully.
- **W0 Non-Goal 7** — no `complian-`, safety, or `certif-` claim arises from anything in this record or from any artefact it governs, in this era or any later one.
- **No new authority.** No edge, zone, class, grant type, authority state, or namespace is minted. **No law required reinterpretation, and no amendment is proposed or required.**

## Non-goals

---

*A fence is a strange thing to be proud of. But the whole wager of this phase is that the interesting part was never the model — it was whether a repository could decide, in advance and in public, exactly what it would refuse to hold, and then hold to that when something fluent and plausible arrived asking to be believed. The words are set now: three origins, one clause, one ceiling, and no pathway for scrubbing. Nothing has been said yet that nobody wrote. When something is, it will land inside a fence that was already here.*
