**Status:** Accepted by human reviewer, 2026-08-17. Not a build instruction. Authorises no implementation.
**Date:** August 2026 · **Phase:** W6 — Surface Era · **Deliverable:** **W6-D1**

It renders nothing, creates no catalogue, allocates no identifier, and grades no string.

---

**The vocabulary said which words exist; the firewall said no word or pixel may create authority. This record does the tender part: it teaches each uncomfortable truth how to stand in public. Unknown gets to remain unknown at full size. Deferred gets its dependency named instead of its dignity taken. An executed trap gets to say a run happened without anyone hearing "passed". The Wing's honest states are the most valuable things it owns — this law is how they survive being looked at.**

## Decision question

**How does each honest state render — unknown, review-required, deferred, `behaviourally_executed`, `evidenced`, `not_evidenced`, applicability-unresolved — such that display preserves exactly the state's own meaning, carries its mandatory non-authority accompaniment, and admits no textual or visual form that improves, softens, completes, shames, or hides it?**

## Decisions

### Part A — The door and the consumption

1. **This record decides rendering law and renders nothing.** No state displays anywhere yet; when one first does, it displays under this law.

2. **Consumption, exact:** ADR-0038 Parts A, B, E and F and ADR-0039 whole are this record's operating frame. Each per-state law below is a specialisation inside that frame — never an exception to it — and no accompaniment, bar, or class assignment consumed here is narrowed by anything here.

### Part B — The per-state rendering laws

3. **Unknown.** Unknown renders as a **standing truth with its basis** — what is unknown, and why the honest answer is unknown (`unknown-not-absent` displays with its recorded basis, exactly as the evaluation records hold it). Unknown **never** renders as absence, a blank, an empty cell, *missing*, an error, a failure, or work to be cleared; no display resolves, ages out, or downgrades an unknown — **display never resolves an unknown, because display resolves nothing**. Where sibling states display, omitting the unknowns among them is the V4 concealment overclaim, barred. Accompaniment: **unknown is a standing truth, not an absence.**

4. **Review-required / review-routed.** Renders as a **route to human review** — a destination and a status, never a judgement: no verdict, no pass/fail, no recommendation, no score, no urgency theatre, and no wording or visual implying the system has approved, rejected, or leaned. It may name where the matter went (*routed to human review*) and its routing status (*awaiting human review*); the detailed presentation of what was routed — deltas, captures, variants — **remains DR-W6-03's**, and nothing here pre-decides it. Accompaniment: **routed for human review — no machine judgement exists or is implied.**

5. **Deferred.** Renders **with its named dependency visible** — the wait and what it waits on, in the dependency's own recorded words. Deferred **never** renders as failure, progress, completion, *blocked by system*, *coming soon*, or a roadmap promise; a governed wait is a state with standing, not a negative result and not a marketing surface. Accompaniment: **deferred on a named dependency — neither a failure nor a completion.**

6. **`behaviourally_executed`.** Renders as **one governed run happened — and nothing more.** It **never** renders as passed, tested, successful, safe, correct, validated, proven, complete, or behaviourally complete; no alias, glyph, colour, grouping, or ordering may present execution as success (V1–V3 and V6 apply with full force, and success-grouping is barred by name). The **executed-is-not-passed accompaniment is mandatory and travels in reach, unabbreviated** (ADR-0038 decision 19, carried). The state's whole displayable meaning is: this governed execution event occurred, its records exist, and its behavioural substance was not judged.

7. **`evidenced`.** Renders as **evidence pointer present — evidence located at the cited source** (with the citation displayed where applicable, per ADR-0038 decision 9's source-bounded prose). It **never** renders as legally satisfied, safe, production-ready, approved, requirement-satisfied, or any word from the `complian-` or `certif-` stems. The standing non-claim is mandatory: **an evidence pointer locates evidence; it never asserts the mapped requirement is satisfied** — displayed with the state, not linked from it.

8. **`not_evidenced`.** Renders as **honest absence of evidence** — a deliberate honesty state with the same dignity as its siblings. It **never** renders as failure, shame, alarm, a warning-by-default, a defect-by-default, or a hidden state; it is **never suppressed where its evidence siblings display** (V4, applied by name); and *source gap* / *no evidence located* phrasings are lawful only inside the non-authority boundary that says absence of evidence asserts nothing beyond itself. Accompaniment: **no evidence is located for this control; absence of evidence is recorded honestly and claims nothing.**

9. **Applicability-unresolved.** Renders as **unresolved applicability, at full size** — the overlay's derived label (*applicability unresolved*) exactly as the Lane C derivation produces it, never a pass, never *not applicable*, never dismissed, never *safe to ignore*, never irrelevant. It stays visible wherever applicability siblings display, and **display preserves unresolved as unresolved** — resolution is a future governed act on the applicability records, and no rendering, grouping, or default filter may perform or imply it. Accompaniment: **applicability is unresolved — unresolved is not "not applicable", and no pass is implied.**

### Part C — The accompaniment table, mandatory

10. **Six accompaniments, one per state family, each a non-authority label under ADR-0038's class and each travelling with its state in reach and unabbreviated:** execution → *executed is not passed* · evidence → *evidence presence is not satisfaction* · applicability → *unresolved is not "not applicable"* · review routing → *human review, not machine verdict* · unknown → *a standing truth, not an absence* · deferred → *a named dependency, not a failure or completion*. **Pending-stub and carried-question accompaniments remain DR-W6-03's**, referenced here only as a boundary. Weakening, abbreviating into an icon, or moving an accompaniment out of reach is a firewall breach (ADR-0039 bind vi), restated here so no rendering decision can treat accompaniments as furniture.

### Part D — Barred forms, textual and visual

11. **Barred textual transformations, summarised from the laws above and barred in every register:** unknown → absence/blank/error · review-routed → verdict/recommendation/score · deferred → failure/progress/completion/promise · executed → passed/tested/successful/safe/correct/validated/proven/complete · evidenced → satisfied/approved/`complian-` or `certif-` forms · not_evidenced → failure/defect/alarm/invisible · unresolved → not-applicable/dismissed/irrelevant. Every one is the same move — renaming a state into what the source does not hold — and ADR-0038 decision 5 already names it: authority by thesaurus.

12. **Barred visual forms, consuming V1–V6 per state:** no green/pass/checkmark/verdict visual on any state here; no traffic-light grading semantics; no success or failure glyphs; no readiness meters; no aggregate all-good surface over any set of these states; no concealment of the uncomfortable members (unknown, deferred, `not_evidenced`, unresolved) where siblings display; no prominence-as-confidence; and no affordance implying conversion, approval, retirement, or resolution without ceremony. **Visual semantics obey every textual rule in this record exactly** (ADR-0039 rule 11, applied).

### Part E — Boundaries

13. **What later records and artefacts consume from this record:** DR-W6-03 consumes the review-routing law (decision 4) as its boundary and owns everything past it; DR-W6-05's catalogue validation consumes decisions 10–12 as string-set and declaration obligations; DR-W6-06's surface contract consumes decisions 3–12 as the per-state conformance set a surface must prove; the W6-D2 catalogue, when its own brief opens it, will carry these accompaniments and rendering rules as catalogue content under its own proofs.

14. **This record does not decide:** review-routed delta presentation in detail, pending-stub surfacing, or carried-open-question surfacing (DR-W6-03) · catalogue identity and the catalogue-ID namespace (DR-W6-05) · the review-surface boundary contract (DR-W6-06) · any catalogue artefact or final catalogue ID · any string grading · any rendered label · any UI or surface implementation.

15. **W6-D1 remains open; W6-D2 through W6-D6 remain unopened; W7 remains unopened.** Nothing here converts a stub, changes a status, touches a fixture, an evaluation record, Lane C, Tier 3 or applicability, contacts a model, or adds a dependency. The twenty-six `unknown-not-absent` records remain unknown; the twenty-three fixtures remain `behaviourally_executed` and not passed; the deferred rows remain deferred; the four applicability records remain unresolved — **this law describes how such states will one day stand in the light, and changes none of them.**

## Alternatives considered

- **Progressive disclosure for uncomfortable states (rejected).** Tucking unknowns and absences behind a click is concealment with extra steps; V4 exists precisely for this.
- **Softening `not_evidenced` into neutral gray silence (rejected).** Invisibility is the polite form of shame. The state displays with standing, dignity, and its own accompaniment.
- **Letting review routing carry a machine "suggestion" (rejected firmly).** A suggestion is a verdict wearing modesty; ADR-0039 bind xi forecloses it structurally.
- **A single generic disclaimer instead of per-state accompaniments (rejected).** One banner absolves nothing and teaches nothing; each state carries its own exact non-claim, in reach.
- **Rendering executed with a neutral "done" mark (rejected, ruled).** "Done" is completion vocabulary; the state means a run happened, and its display says exactly that.

## Consequences

- Every honest state now has a worked public posture: what it says, what must travel with it, and what may never be done to it — before any of them is ever shown.
- The catalogue and surfaces inherit rendering rules as data-shaped obligations, not taste; DR-W6-03 inherits a clean boundary at the routing label.
- The Wing's most distinctive property — doubts displayed at full size — is now law rather than intention.
