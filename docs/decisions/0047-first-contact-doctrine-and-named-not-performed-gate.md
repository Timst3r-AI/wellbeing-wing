# 0047 — First-Contact Doctrine and the Named-Not-Performed Gate (DR-W7-02)

**Status:** Accepted by human reviewer, 2026-08-19. Not a build instruction. Authorises no implementation by itself.
**Date:** August 2026 · **Phase:** W7 — First-Contact Governance and Synthetic Model Evaluation · **Deliverable:** **W7-D1** (the **second and final** of two D1 decision records)
**Position:** the crossing, named and not made. Lands **after** ADR-0046 because boundary-before-contact binds, and consumes that record's fence whole rather than re-deciding any part of it. It defines what model contact *is*, what must be true before it may occur, and why it cannot be undone once it has — and it **performs no contact, authorises none, schedules none, and makes none inevitable.**
**Constitutional references:** W0 Laws 1, 3, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13; W0 §10; W0 Non-Goal 7. **No law is amended.**
**North star carried:** generated text may enter the public Wing only as synthetic, governed evaluation evidence — never authority, advice, truth, safety evidence, approval, or a decision about any person.
**Resolves:** none.

---

**A crossing that is never described is crossed by accident. That is the whole reason this record exists before anything is transmitted anywhere: to say precisely what the act is, so that doing it can only ever be a separate, authorised, recorded decision — and so that nobody can arrive at it sideways, through a convenient definition, a routing detail, or a deliverable that seemed to require it. Six phases have not heard a sentence they did not write. This record does not change that. It writes down what would change it, sets seven conditions that must all hold first, and records the one property that makes the whole exercise necessary: a generated sentence cannot be un-generated. The gate is named. It is not opened.**

## Decision question

**What exactly is model contact, how is it distinguished from generated output and from an authored synthetic specimen, what must all be true before it may occur, what follows from the fact that it cannot be undone, and what does it mean for W7 to name the crossing without performing it — decided now, as fixed input to W7-D2 through W7-D7, without contacting anything, generating anything, authoring any specimen, or narrowing the model-boundary options reserved to W7-D3?**

## Decisions

### Part A — The door

1. **This record names a crossing and does not make it.** After its landing the repository contains exactly one new governed artefact: this record. **No model has been contacted, no text has been generated or imported, no specimen has been authored, no record of the generated-output class exists, no schema field is defined, no harness exists, and no dependency is added.** Every statement below about contact describes an act that only a separate, later, authorised landing could perform.

2. **ADR-0046 is consumed, not reopened.** Where this record refers to the fence — origins, exclusions, the boundary invariant, the non-naming law, declarability, the ceiling, specimen parity, no-recirculation, finding-is-an-event — it cites ADR-0046 and adds nothing to it. **A later record may extend those provisions through its own ceremony; this one does not, and no reading of this record may be used to narrow them.**

### Part B — The three terms, defined by provenance

3. **The definitions are by provenance, never by vendor, medium, or transport.** A definition keyed to *who supplies the model*, *what protocol carries the request*, or *whether the exchange is interactive* can be satisfied by re-routing. A definition keyed to **where the text came from** cannot. The three terms are closed and mutually exclusive:

   - **(a) Model contact** — **the act of transmitting any probe, prompt, fixture, or governed material to a model and receiving text back.** Local or hosted, direct or adapted, interactive or batched, one exchange or many, by script or by hand. **This is the crossing.** It is the act, not the artefact: contact occurs at transmission-and-return, whether or not anything is kept.
   - **(b) Generated output** — **text produced by a model.** The artefact class governed by `W7-AR` §6's nine binding properties, whose shape is W7-D2's and exists nowhere yet.
   - **(c) Authored synthetic specimen** — **text written by a human inside this repository, from nothing, deliberately carrying the properties of generated prose**, for the purpose of exercising the governed handling. Origin (a) under ADR-0046 decision 7.

4. **A specimen is not model contact, and authoring one is not performing the crossing.** A specimen has a human author inside this repository; contact has a model outside it. **The distinction is provenance and nothing else** — not quality, not plausibility, not whether a reader could tell them apart. A specimen indistinguishable from generated output is still not generated output, and a generated sentence trivially rewritten by a human is still generated output. **Provenance does not launder in either direction.**

5. **Nothing in decision 4 makes specimens a lesser case.** It makes them a *lawful* case. Whether public W7 ever uses specimens at all is **W7-D3's** decision under `W7-AR` §7 option D, and this record neither prefers nor forecloses it.

### Part C — The governed handling binds on all three

6. **The governed-handling doctrine binds on generated output and on authored synthetic specimens identically.** Record shape, paired-variant capture whole, no machine-selected winner, no omitted variant, human routing of every delta, the non-authority ceiling, provenance declaration, and the public-safety posture apply in full to both. This extends ADR-0046 decision 25's specimen parity from the ceiling to the whole handling regime.

7. **Why, stated so no later deliverable treats it as ceremony:** **the pattern is proven by the handling, not by the provenance of the text being handled.** A phase that handled specimens loosely because "nothing real was said" would have proven nothing about how it handles what a model says. **The handling is the claim. The text is only the occasion** — which is the runway's thesis in its operative form: *the model is not the milestone.*

8. **Consequently, a weaker regime for specimens is not available at any later gate.** No deliverable may propose reduced capture, optional routing, an abbreviated ceiling, or relaxed declaration on the ground that the text was authored rather than generated. **Such a proposal is a stop-and-report event, not a scoping choice.**

11. **The set is conjunctive and closed.** No precondition may be waived, deemed inapplicable, satisfied by analogy, or treated as met because the others are. **A future record may add an eighth; none may remove one**, and removal by re-interpretation is removal.

### Part E — One-way, non-retroactive, and stop-and-report

12. **First contact is one-way.** Once performed it cannot be un-performed. A generated sentence cannot be un-generated by deleting the file that holds it, reverting the commit that landed it, or declining to publish it. **The act is complete at transmission-and-return**, and every artefact question afterwards is about what is *kept*, never about whether it *happened*.

13. **Therefore the gate is checked before, never after, and there is no retroactive authorisation path.** No landing, ruling, or record may authorise a contact that has already occurred. **Retroactive authorisation does not exist in this repository for this act**, and a record purporting to grant it would be void as to the contact it names.

14. **A generated byte appearing before its gate is a stop-and-report event.** Under `W7-AR` §10 it halts the work in hand and is reported to the human authority. **It is never a record to be regularised, back-dated, quietly discarded, or accepted with an apology attached** — and the report states plainly that contact occurred, because decision 12 means the alternative is a false statement.

15. **The honest consequence, recorded rather than smoothed:** if contact ever occurs outside these preconditions, this doctrine cannot undo it. **What it can do is guarantee the event is visible, named, and impossible to describe as authorised.** That is the whole protection a one-way act admits of, and claiming more would be the kind of comfort this repository does not offer itself.

### Part F — Two principles consumed from ADR-0046, applied to contact

16. **No-recirculation, consumed from ADR-0046 decision 9 and not re-decided.** Capture is terminal. Applied to contact, it means: **the output of a contact may be captured into a record of the class and may never become an input to another prompt, probe, fixture, catalogue string, doctrine record, summary, or further contact.** There is no chained-exchange path, no conversation-continuation path, and no self-feeding evaluation loop — not because they are difficult, but because each would make a captured sentence an origin, and ADR-0046 decision 8 makes a fourth origin unlawful.

17. **Finding-is-an-event, consumed from ADR-0046 decision 13 and not re-decided.** A scan finding inside generated or specimen text is a governed event with a disposition path, never a silent allowlisting. Applied to contact: **the disposition is decided before the text is kept, not after it is inconvenient to discard.** **The mechanism is W7-D2's**, wholly, and must be designed inside this principle rather than around it. Naming the assignment here is deliberate: silence would read as a decision, and it is not one.

### Part G — The first-contact anti-collapse chain

18. **The chain, normative and non-collapsible**, extending ADR-0033 decision 21 and ADR-0034 decision 19 and weakening neither:

> **named ≠ authorised · authorised ≠ scheduled · scheduled ≠ performed · specimen ≠ contact · contact ≠ capability · generated ≠ said · fluent ≠ correct · plausible ≠ true · two variants ≠ a better variant · difference ≠ defect · captured ≠ endorsed · routed ≠ reviewed · reviewed ≠ approved · model output ≠ evidence · no contact ≠ nothing proven.**

19. **No record, runtime, summary, surface, packet, or closure may collapse any link into another**, and the chain is carried whole wherever it is carried — abbreviation is collapse by other means.

20. **The last link is load-bearing in both directions, and is the one most likely to be misread.** Choosing not to contact a model proves nothing about a model — **and it does not mean W7 proved nothing.** The governed handling is the claim, and the handling can be proven whole without a model ever being contacted. **A closure record that treats a no-contact outcome as a failure to deliver would have collapsed this link.**

### Part H — The named-not-performed gate

21. **W7 names model contact. This record does not perform it, authorise it, schedule it, require it, or make it inevitable.** Naming a crossing is how this repository makes a crossing deliberate: described precisely enough that stepping over it can only be a separate, authorised, recorded act, and precisely enough that stepping over it *by accident* becomes structurally hard rather than merely discouraged.

22. **This record narrows nothing that belongs to W7-D3.** The model-boundary decision and its four options — repository-managed local dependency, adapter to an externally running local model, manual governed import, and no public model contact with authored synthetic specimens — **remain wholly W7-D3's, to be taken from the sources through a Tier F crossing record.** Nothing here prefers, ranks, forecloses, or pre-positions any of them, and **no reading of this record may be offered as support for one.** Defining what contact *is* is not a step toward doing it.

23. **The dormant doors stay dormant.** ADR-0033's hosted class stays closed and its local class doctrinally constructable only; **naming the crossing opens neither, and neither is opened by implication, preparation, or the existence of a definition.** E10, Z4 and E12/Z5 are untouched.

### Part I — Proof obligations

24. **Four obligations are specified. Two are mechanical, one is review-only in full, and one waits on an artefact class that does not exist.** Each names what fails it, and the decidability column is a claim this record is accountable for — **the ADR-0046 experience binds: an obligation is not called mechanical until a working check has been demonstrated against real bytes.**

| # | Obligation | Fails when | Decidability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1** | **Chain integrity.** The fifteen-link chain of decision 18 is carried byte-identically wherever it appears in a governed record. | A link is dropped, reordered, reworded, or the chain is abbreviated. | **Mechanical** |
| **Q2** | **Precondition-set integrity.** The decision 9 table carries exactly seven preconditions, each with a source and a named discharge location. | The count changes, a discharge location is emptied, or a row loses its source. | **Mechanical** |
| **Q3** | **No record asserts that contact has occurred while precondition 3 stands undischarged.** | Any record, packet, summary, or board row states or implies that model contact took place. | **Review-only, in full.** Whether a sentence asserts contact is a semantic judgement, exactly as ADR-0046 decision 29(b) found for elaboration; no pattern separates *describing the crossing* from *reporting having made it* |
| **Q4** | **Gate precedence.** No artefact of the generated-output class exists without an accepted record shape, and no contact artefact exists without an accepted model boundary. | Either artefact precedes its gate. | **Mechanical, but not yet implementable** — it checks a class that does not exist, and a proof over an empty set is a proof of nothing |

25. **Implementation timing, and the self-reference question settled in advance rather than at implementation.** **Q1 and Q2 are implementable at this record's own landing**, since their subject matter is this record's own published bytes; both were demonstrated working against a draft of this record before either was called mechanical, which is the discipline ADR-0046's P1 experience bought.

   **Q1's module faces the P1 self-reference problem, and has two lawful answers.** A module that checks a canonical string must hold that string, and a module that proves the check *bites* must also hold a deliberately broken variant — which the check would then find. **Either (a) the module holds the chain literally and excludes only itself**, exactly as ADR-0046 decision 28 records for P1, with the exclusion asserted to be one path resolving to the module; **or (b) the module constructs the chain and its controls from escape sequences**, so no literal anchor appears in its source and no exclusion is needed. (a) keeps the canonical chain readable in the file that guards it and reuses an established precedent; (b) avoids an exclusion at the cost of making the guarded text unreadable where it is guarded.

   **Mechanism (a) is chosen, and is disclosed here rather than after the fact.** The Q1 and Q2 proofs land at `tests/test_w7_first_contact_doctrine.py`, which holds the fifteen-link chain **literally** so that the canonical text stays legible in the file that guards it, together with a deliberately broken variant that proves the check bites. Because both appear in its source, the module excludes **only itself** from its own scan scope through `SELF_EXCLUDED = frozenset({"tests/test_w7_first_contact_doctrine.py"})`; the exclusion is asserted to be exactly that one path and to resolve to the module itself, so it cannot widen silently. **No other artefact is excluded, and Q2 is unaffected because it reads this record directly.** An undisclosed scope boundary in a green check is the failure ADR-0046 decision 30 names, and this is the disclosure that prevents it.

26. **Q3 is never implementable, and no green result is evidence for it.** It joins P2 as a **standing human-review duty** at this landing and every later W7 landing, discharged by no test, no scan, and no suite. **Q1 and Q2 green together prove that the chain is intact and the preconditions are enumerated. They prove nothing whatever about whether contact occurred**, and no packet, board row, closure record, or summary may imply otherwise. **Q4 remains a debt until the artefact class exists.**

### Part J — Alternatives, assessed

27. **Defining contact by vendor or by transport (rejected).** Key the definition to a named provider, an API, a protocol, or an interactive session. Rejected: every such definition is satisfiable by re-routing, and a gate that a change of transport walks around is not a gate. Provenance is the only property that does not move when the plumbing does.

28. **Treating an authored specimen as a lesser artefact (rejected).** Permit reduced capture, optional routing, or an abbreviated ceiling for text a human wrote. Rejected under decisions 6–8: it would prove the handling against the easy case and leave the hard one untested, and it would make the no-contact option a way of *avoiding* the phase's obligations rather than a lawful way of *meeting* them.

29. **A retroactive-authorisation path for good-faith accidents (rejected).** Allow a record to bless a contact that already happened where no harm is evident. Rejected: it would convert a one-way act into a reversible one on paper while leaving it one-way in fact, and it would make the gate advisory. **The honest alternative — visibility — is decision 14, and it is the one adopted.**

30. **Deciding the model boundary here, since the definitions are being written anyway (rejected on authority, not on preference).** `W7-AR` §7 reserves that decision to W7-D3 and forbids deciding it by momentum. **This record has no view to record**, and recording one would itself be the failure the runway names.

32. **What this record hands W7-D3:** the seven preconditions with their discharge locations, of which W7-D3 owns precondition 2 and must name precondition 3's gate if its decision permits contact at all — **together with an option space this record has left exactly as wide as it found it.**

33. **What this record hands W7-D5 through W7-D7:** the anti-collapse chain to carry whole, the standing review duties Q3 and P2, and decision 20's warning that a no-contact outcome is not a failure to deliver.

34. **What this record does not touch.** ADR-0046's fence, in any provision. The pending ledger, the carried W6 findings, the applicability records, the Tier 3 rows, the T12 obligation, and the carried questions. E10, Z4, E12/Z5, the hosted class, and the local class. W7-D2 through W7-D7 remain unopened, and **W8 is not named as anything but unopened.**

## Consequences

**Made easier:** W7-D3 inherits a decision rather than a definitional argument — what contact *is* is settled, so the option debate can be about substance instead of scope. W7-D2 can design one record shape for two provenances rather than two shapes with a boundary between them. And any future proposal that would reach a model by a novel route is already classified before it is argued.

**Made harder, deliberately:** there is no chained-exchange or conversation-continuation path, so any evaluation design that assumed one must be rebuilt. Specimens cost exactly as much governance as generated text, which removes the cheap version of the no-contact option. And the seven preconditions are conjunctive with no waiver, so the slowest one sets the pace.

**Accepted cost, stated in the ADR-0046 form.** **One obligation can never be mechanised** — Q3 joins P2 as a permanent review-only duty, which means the two questions that matter most in this phase, *was the authority named* and *did contact occur*, are both guarded by human attention alone. **One waits** — Q4 is a debt until the class exists. **And the doctrine cannot prevent the act it governs**; decision 15 says so plainly rather than implying otherwise.

*The strange thing about naming a crossing is how much it looks like preparing to make one. It is the opposite. Everything written here makes the act harder, slower, more visible, and more clearly someone's decision — and the last line of the chain exists so that if the answer turns out to be that no model is ever contacted in public at all, nobody can call that a failure.
