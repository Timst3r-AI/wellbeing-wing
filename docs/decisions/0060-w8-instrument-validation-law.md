## Part D — Families and the session rule

14. **A family is a set of two or three cases built from one seed text, each carrying that seed byte-identically as one of its two variants.** Families are optional. **Any variant text that two or more validation cases share must be one declared family seed;** undeclared sharing is a construction fault, refused at authoring and at reveal. Shared text is visible in the published cases; which cases form a declared family, and by which methods, stays withheld until reveal.
15. **Exactly one validation session is held, over the whole corpus, family members included.** Family members are recognisable to a reader even while their relation is withheld, and separating them across sessions would not remove the exposure already acquired at the corpus-candidate review, or through the reviewer's own earlier tokens. **The session record states this co-presence rule plainly, without disclosing whether any family exists.** Case independence carries whole: each case is dispositioned on its own two variants alone, and no case is decided because of another (ADR-0054 decision 30).
