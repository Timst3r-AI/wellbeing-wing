## Decision question

**How may the Wing define behavioural evaluation architecture — the harness boundary, the observation standard, the meaning of results, the `execution_status` vocabulary and its transition governance, re-run semantics, false-positive and false-negative controls, and the T12 condition seam — without converting tests into truth, model output into evidence, fixture execution into implementation authority, or evaluation success into permission to promote, persist, transmit, diagnose, infer, or act?**

# Part A — Behavioural evaluation constructability

## A1. The door decision

1. **This record decides behavioural evaluation architecture, and nothing downstream of it.** It executes no fixture, builds no harness, contacts no model, converts no test, creates no behavioural proof, and authorises no W5-D2, W5-D3 or W5-D4 implementation. Accepted doctrine gates capability; it never is capability.

2. **Behavioural evaluation is governed observation of system behaviour under synthetic fixture conditions.** It exists because OR-3 is true and permanent: topology closes channels, and a model can still err inside a lawful one. Evaluation is the only mitigation for that class of failure, which makes this architecture constitutionally necessary — and makes its honesty discipline non-negotiable.

3. **Evaluation observes behaviour. It does not prove truth, does not mint authority, does not certify safety, does not prove clinical reliability, and does not make any system fit for uncontrolled use.** Every downstream artefact, record, summary and sentence is bound by this decision.

## A2. Two instruments, never substituted

4. **Deterministic before generative, always** (W1-D6 §3.2; W5-AR §2). Where a rule can be checked mechanically it is checked mechanically; behavioural evaluation is reserved for what topology cannot decide. **Structural unreachability is proven first, or the behavioural result is not evidence of architectural closure** — a behavioural result over a still-open channel measures model discretion, not architecture.

5. **Neither instrument substitutes for the other.** A green structural proof is never presented as behavioural evidence (ADR-0024 decision 23), and a green behavioural run is never presented as structural proof. The deterministic suite and the behavioural instrument answer different questions and their results are never merged into one claim.

## A3. Fixture doctrine — carried, not re-decided

6. **A fixture is inert synthetic data: a well-made trap, never a test result.** W4-D6-BEF governs fixture structure, identity, families, probes, mapping, validation and maintenance **whole and unamended**, as direct authority. This record adds no fixture, edits no fixture, and re-decides nothing W4-D6-BEF settled.

7. **A green fixture corpus means the traps are well-formed and referenced — nothing more.** The corpus validator's output mints no doctrine and describes no behaviour.

8. **A fixture file never holds a behavioural result.** The §7 schema rule and the fixed `result_location` are carried in full: results live in future governed evaluation records only, never in fixture files, and no schema extension may add a result field.

9. **Fixture maintenance stays under W4-D6-BEF §12** — governed updates through ceremony, visible supersession, no silent edits, and the map as the authoritative live record.

## A4. Synthetic-only discipline, extended to every evaluation artefact

10. **Every evaluation artefact is synthetic-only: fixtures, harness prompts, transcripts, harness output, observation records, and evaluation records.** No real user content, no real room state, no real health content, no real grants, no real consent — **and no evaluation channel to a person exists or may be created.** The W2-D4 discipline binds by construction, never by scrubbing, and W5-AR §10 extends it to runtime artefacts unchanged.

11. **The W2-D4 §8 deferral is resolved here at doctrine level.** W2-D4 barred behavioural-evaluation transcripts and prompts pending *"their own strategy extension, reviewed then."* W4-D6-BEF supplied that extension for fixtures; **this record supplies it for harness prompts and execution transcripts: they are governed evaluation artefacts under the W2-D4 synthetic discipline and the public-safety scan, exactly as documents are.** Their format, templates and authoring are W5-D3's, within this meaning and never outside it.

12. **No real grant exists anywhere in evaluation.** A grant delegates a real user's authority (ADR-0030; W1-D2); evaluation has no user. **Fixture grant-shapes are data, never consents**; the consent grammar is untouched; rights are untouched; and any future evaluation involving a real person's content would require its own separate governed authority, which nothing here creates or anticipates.

## A5. The harness boundary contract

13. **The harness is defined as a boundary contract, not a build.** The architecture must be able to: **consume fixture data as the sole scenario source** · **execute paired variants where required** (A6, B2) · **observe all four required surfaces** (B1) · **record every observation in governed evaluation artefacts** (B6) · **distinguish what it decides mechanically from what it routes to human review** · **carry false-positive and false-negative controls** · **preserve honest unknown** · and **never rely on output-only evaluation.**

14. **The harness may never:** fetch from any governed user store · touch real user content, real room state or real grants · create, raise or launder authority · write user, room, profile or vault state · treat its own output, or any model's output, as ground truth · or emit content-bearing telemetry outside governed evaluation artefacts.

15. **Mechanical versus routed-to-review is a boundary, not a preference.** The harness decides mechanically only what is mechanical — schema conformance, and the presence or absence of stated deltas against stated criteria. **Every judgment call routes to human review, and the harness never grades itself:** its false-positive and false-negative controls are part of what W5-D3 must build and prove, and their absence makes the instrument unfit to run.

16. **This record does not decide:** harness code · framework · prompts · system prompts · transcript format · model provider · SDK · client · credential · runtime directory · storage schema · or execution method. Each is W5-D3's or W5-D2's, behind its own gates.

## A6. The first-contact boundary

17. **No model contact is authorised by this record**, and none has ever occurred. Any model-contacting harness proof requires, together: **W5-D2 runtime authority** · **every applicable Tier F crossing** · **a named first-contact gate in the W5-D3 brief** · **synthetic-only artefacts throughout** · and **no hosted model access unless a future governed record opens that class.** This record defines the boundary and may not cross it.

18. **Evaluation-era instrument access remains exactly ADR-0033's category:** lawful as a category, with method, prompts, providers, fixtures, execution, observation records and test conversion all governed here and executed only under W5-D3/W5-D4's own gates.

## A7. The evaluation anti-collapse chain

19. **The chain, normative and non-collapsible:** **fixture ≠ result · trap ≠ catch · well-formed fixture ≠ behavioural proof · executed ≠ passed · passed ≠ safe · observed ≠ proven · repeated ≠ confirmed · model output ≠ evidence · evaluation record ≠ evidence about a person · harness green ≠ implementation authority · instrument ≠ runtime · architecture ≠ execution.** No runtime, record, summary or surface may collapse any link into another.

# Part B — Observation, ground truth, pass/fail and non-authority

## B1. The four surfaces

20. **The observation standard is the four surfaces, carried from W4-D6-BEF §8 and ADR-0024 decision 13:** **spoken output · persisted state · routing and propagation · behaviour selection, ranking, framing, and omission.** **Output-only evaluation is structurally insufficient: a harness that classifies spoken output alone cannot satisfy the contract**, and no architecture choice may quietly reduce the standard to the one surface that is easy to read.

21. **Observability belongs to the boundary; the instrument reads what the boundary exposes.** The harness recovers nothing the adapter was not built to show (ADR-0024 decision 13), and observation never becomes a second data channel (decision 14).

## B2. Paired variants

22. **Paired variants are mandatory wherever the claim depends on contrast** — absence/presence, silent/overt, cross-room reach, inference, or laundering — which is every silent-channel probe the corpus carries and every fixture family whose pass condition is a behavioural delta. **This record does not state that every conceivable behavioural test must use paired variants**; it states that where the claim is a delta claim, the contrast is the evidence, and an unpaired delta claim is not evidence.

23. **Where paired variants apply, the fixture's own `context_present_variant` and `context_absent_variant` and the W4-D6-BEF pass condition govern**, and the harness must construct and control both variants — a construction requirement the boundary already supports by design.

## B3. Ground truth and calibration

24. **Ground truth is the governed record and the fixture's stated expectation — never a model's opinion, never a downstream model summary, and never a human impression unsupported by the governed record.** The prohibited and required outcome classes written into each fixture are the only standard a result is read against.

25. **Model output is never evidence and never ground truth for tests** (ADR-0033, carried in terms). Nothing inside the instrument may substitute any model's judgment for the governed record, and anything a model produces during evaluation arrives labelled agent-origin and stays that way.

26. **Grader calibration is symmetric by doctrine:** underclaim and overclaim are scored with equal weight, and **honest unknown is rewarded where it is the governed answer** — a system penalised for honest uncertainty will learn to stop reporting it (W1-D6 §3.4–3.5; D5-T08). Any grading mechanism W5-D3 builds carries this calibration as an acceptance condition, not a tuning choice.

## B4. What a pass means

27. **A pass means exactly this and nothing more: a bounded system path behaved as expected under a named fixture, on the observed surfaces, under stated conditions.**

28. **A pass never means, and may never be presented as:** safe · truthful · clinically reliable · therapeutic · certified · fit for uncontrolled use · approved for production · or authorised to persist, promote, transmit, diagnose, infer or act. **W0 Non-Goal 7 binds every evaluation sentence:** no evaluation result is a compliance, safety, health or certification claim, anywhere, ever.

29. **A failed probe is an observation, recorded honestly.** It is evidence that the trap caught the move under stated conditions; it routes to review; it is never a verdict about a person, and it is never tidied away — the ADR-0032 honesty discipline reaches evaluation records in full.

## B5. Non-determinism

30. **Under a non-deterministic instrument, a result is a bounded observational claim over stated repeated trials with disclosed variance — never an equality claim, never a universal behavioural claim, and never safety certification.** *"No behavioural delta"* under non-determinism is a statistical statement and must be presented as one.

31. **Trial counts, statistics, thresholds and variance rules are W5-D3's, within decision 30's meaning.** This answers this record's half of the jointly held determinism question (ADR-0024 open question 1); ADR-0033's half remains what it was — constraint-free carriage that forecloses no instrument the boundary can support.

## B6. The evaluation artefact class

32. **A distinct governed artefact class is created at doctrine level: evaluation records** — comprising execution observations, observation records, and transcripts. **It is not C0 by default** (evaluation records carry synthetic scenario content and model output, not content-free metadata); it is never stored in fixture files; and **no schema, table, storage mechanism, retention mechanics or event-store design is prescribed here.**

33. **The never-become rules bind the class permanently:** never processing input · never a profiling source · never cross-room analytics · **never a behavioural dataset about a person** · never an authority source · **never clinical evidence** · **never safety certification.**

34. **Evaluation records exist to be examined.** Governed evaluation may read, analyse and score them for the authorised W5 evaluation purpose (ADR-0024 decision 14, carried) — a record no one may examine would prove nothing — and they are **scanned exactly as documents are, synthetic-only, with no exemption for machine production.**

35. **Home, retention and lifecycle are governed at W5-D4's own landing, within decisions 32–34.** Stating that assignment here is deliberate: silence would otherwise read as a decision, and it is not one.

## B7. Telemetry and logging

36. **Harness logs, transcripts, telemetry and diagnostics are governed surfaces under ADR-0032's mechanics, carried whole:** not exempt because operational, no content-bearing telemetry outside governed evaluation artefacts, no hidden SDK defaults, no silent retry, and no comfort claims about any of it. **Whether a running instrument keeps its telemetry honest is precisely the question ADR-0032 decision 57 assigned here — and the architecture's answer is that telemetry honesty is a W5-D3 proof obligation, not an assumption.**

37. **No evaluation result may be presented as a safety claim, a health claim, or a certification of any kind** (W5-AR §10) — including in telemetry, dashboards, summaries, README rows, or any derived artefact, in this era or any later one.

# Part C — Harness boundaries, pending ledger, execution ownership and future gates

## C1. `execution_status` — vocabulary and transition governance

38. **`execution_status` is execution-state only, and never encodes a result.** The fixture-era starting value remains **`behaviourally_unexecuted`**, exactly as every one of the twenty-three fixtures carries it today.

39. **The W5-era vocabulary is closed at exactly two values: `behaviourally_unexecuted` and `behaviourally_executed`.** Extending it requires its own future governed record. **Barred from the vocabulary by name:** `executed_pass` · `executed_fail` · `passed` · `failed` · `conforming` · `nonconforming` · `safe` · `unsafe` — and any other value that would smuggle an outcome into a status field.

40. **Outcome classes live only in governed evaluation records.** Results never enter fixture files (W5-AR; W4-D6-BEF §7), and **every status transition is a W5-D4-era governed ceremony event recorded per W4-D6-BEF §12 rule 4 — never an edit.** This record performs no transition and changes no status.

41. **Re-run semantics, at doctrine level:** a re-run is a **new governed execution event with its own evaluation record**. Later executions never overwrite, erase or amend earlier evaluation records; the status field remains execution-state only regardless of how many runs exist; and which execution a claim refers to is always stated. Re-run tooling and mechanics are W5-D3/W5-D4's, within this meaning.

## C2. The T12 seam — dispositioned

42. **The disposition, chosen in ink: amendment through ceremony.** The current T12 pending condition — *"room isolation model implemented at the adapter layer"* — **is incomplete as written**: necessary, and not sufficient, because T12 is a behavioural test that also requires the behavioural evaluation machinery its condition does not name. **The condition must be amended through a later governed pending-ledger landing so the ledger states both prerequisites.** **This record does not edit the pending ledger**; it decides the representation and assigns the edit to the first lawful W5-era landing that touches the pending ledger through ceremony.

43. **Until that future landing, and until the real prerequisites actually exist, no conversion may occur under the literal condition.** A stub converted on a literal reading of a condition known to be incomplete would be exactly the honest-looking pass the ledger exists to prevent.

## C3. The pending ledger

44. **The relevant stubs, named:** `test_D5_T05_repetition_resistance_behavioural` and `test_D5_T06_authority_laundering_resistance` (both pending on *"model access is decided and behavioural harness exists"* — the first conjunct satisfied by ADR-0033, the second implementation); `test_D5_T12_cross_room_isolation_behavioural` (the C2 seam); and `test_D5_T13_in_room_silent_inference_resistance` (pending on *"room contracts accepted and behavioural harness exists"* — the first conjunct long true, the second implementation).

45. **DR-W5-07 acceptance satisfies no pending conjunct.** *"Behavioural harness exists"* means an implemented, proven instrument — W5-D3's work, not doctrine's. **All nine pending stubs remain pending, all twenty-three fixtures remain `behaviourally_unexecuted`, and this record performs no test conversion, no pending-ledger edit, and no fixture status change.**

## C4. Ownership

## C5. Local/hosted and the untouched doors

An instrument is not a side door, and nothing in this record widens any boundary any prior record closed or left dormant.

## C6. Deterministic obligations for W5-D3, claiming no behavioural result

48. **Structural obligations, provable without a model:** the harness consumes fixture data as its sole scenario source, with no interface to any governed user store · every observation lands in a governed evaluation artefact · fixture files carry no result field and the status field cannot hold a barred value · paired-variant construction is supported wherever B2 requires it · the four surfaces are read from what the boundary exposes, with no fifth channel invented and no surface dropped · calibration controls exist for both failure directions · judgment-call routing to human review exists and is recorded.

49. **Nothing here is behavioural proof, and stating this architecture claims no behavioural result.** The corpus remains unexecuted; the walls remain unmeasured; and the phase is no closer to a safety claim than it was before this record — by design.

## Alternatives considered

- **One record in three parts (chosen).** Constructability, observation meaning and execution governance are one architecture: a harness contract with no result semantics polices nothing, and result semantics with no harness contract grade an instrument nobody bounded.
- **Deciding a minimal harness implementation now (rejected).** It would resolve implementation questions by doctrine and pre-empt the W5-D3 brief, the exact failure the era seam exists to prevent.
- **Output-only evaluation (rejected firmly).** W4-D6-BEF forecloses it in terms; three of the four surfaces cannot be recovered from outside a boundary built output-first.
- **A result-bearing `execution_status` (rejected, ruled).** `executed_pass` in a fixture-adjacent field is a result in a fixture file by another name — the drift W4-D6-BEF §11 exists to prevent. Status says whether; evaluation records say what.
- **Leaving the T12 condition literal and governing the gap only in doctrine (considered, not chosen).** Workable but weaker: the ledger is the project's honesty instrument, and a condition known to be incomplete should eventually say so in place. **Amendment through ceremony is the ruled disposition**, with the identical interim no-conversion bar either way.
- **Classifying evaluation records as C0 (rejected).** C0 is content-free governance metadata; evaluation records carry synthetic scenario content and model output. Misclassifying them would either break C0's meaning or launder content into a metadata class.
- **A model as grader of record (rejected).** W1-D6 §3.3 is categorical: ground truth is the governed record, never a model's opinion. Any model-assisted detection W5-D3 ever proposes arrives behind its own review, under decision 26's calibration, and is never ground truth.
- **A universal paired-variant mandate (rejected).** Paired variants are the evidence form for delta claims; mandating them for every conceivable test would confuse the form with the discipline.
- **Claiming W0 Open Question 12 as resolved (rejected, ruled).** The conservative `resolves` discipline holds: no registered identifier is squarely closed by this record alone.

## Consequences

- **The W5-D1 doctrine set is complete at seven records**, and W5-D2 milestone briefs may now proceed against accepted doctrine — each behind its own gates, as always.
- **W5-D3 inherits an instrument charter rather than a blank page:** the contract, the calibration obligations, the first-contact gate, and the proof burdens are all named before a line of harness exists.
- **W5-D4 inherits a status vocabulary that cannot lie** and an evaluation-record class whose prohibitions are older than its schema.
- **Harder, deliberately:** the harness cannot grade itself, judgment routes to humans, and an uncomfortable result is recorded as an uncomfortable result — the instrument's honesty is a proof obligation, not a hope.
- **Nothing is proven.** Twenty-three traps remain set and unsprung, nine stubs remain pending, and the record that completes the doctrine era ends by claiming exactly no behavioural result.

## Constitutional check

- **Law 1** — the instrument contacts no one, evaluates nothing in the background, and runs only inside governed, human-initiated evaluation work; no notification surface exists or is implied.

- **W0 Non-Goal 7** — no compliance, safety or certification claim arises from any evaluation, anywhere, in any era.
