# 0041 — Human-Routing Presentation Law (DR-W6-03)

**Status:** Accepted by human reviewer, 2026-08-17. Not a build instruction. Authorises no implementation.
**Date:** August 2026 · **Phase:** W6 — Surface Era · **Deliverable:** **W6-D1**
**Position:** the fourth W6-D1 doctrine record, carrying the brief's laws 10–12: how human-routed material is presented — review-routed deltas, pending stubs, and carried open questions — beyond the routing boundary ADR-0040 decision 4 fixed. It renders nothing, performs no review, records no human act, converts nothing, and retires nothing.
**Constitutional references:** W0 Laws 1, 3, 11, 13; W0 Non-Goal 7. **No law is amended.**
**North star carried:** governed truth must survive display without becoming authority.
**Resolves:** none.

---

**Some of the Wing's records exist precisely because a machine refused to judge them: deltas routed to review, stubs that wait for ceremony, questions carried alive across five phases. This record is the law of showing that material to the person it was routed to — whole, unranked, unconverted, and unretired — because the entire value of routing something to a human is destroyed the moment the display quietly does the human's job first.**

## Decision question

**How are review-routed deltas, pending stubs, and carried open questions shown to a human — with everything the source records hold and nothing they do not — such that display never selects, scores, recommends, converts, retires, clears, or approves, and a human act appears only where a governed source actually records one?**

## Controlling law

- **The accepted W6-D1 brief** — laws 10–12 assigned here; the sequencing note placing this record inside the firewall after ADR-0040.
- **ADR-0038, consumed at the routing and future-owned label classes and V5** — routing labels name destinations, never judgements; future-owned labels say dormant when a thing is dormant; and no affordance may imply an act only ceremony can perform.
- **ADR-0039, consumed whole** — the formula (**display may preserve source authority; display may not create authority**), bind (x) no ceremony-bypassing affordance, and above all **bind (xi): no machine verdict, structurally** — a verdict displays only where a governed source holds a recorded human-authorised act, and no current W6 source holds one.
- **ADR-0040 decision 4** — the review-routing boundary this record begins at: destination and status only; everything past that boundary is decided here. **ADR-0040 decision 10** — the accompaniment table whose pending-stub and carried-question seats this record now fills.
- **The pending ledger's no-bulk-approve obligation** (carried since W4, bound visually by ADR-0038 V5) and **ADR-0034 decisions 15 and 29** — every judgment call routes to human review, the harness never grades itself, and a failed or routed observation is never tidied away.
- **The W5-D4 evaluation posture** — routed deltas exist as paired-variant captures with recorded reasons, and `unknown-not-absent` is a standing truth; **the W4/W5 closure inventories** — the three W6-owned stubs and the carried open questions this law will one day put on a screen.

## Decisions

### Part A — The door and the consumption

1. **This record decides presentation law for human-routed material and presents nothing.** No delta, stub, or question displays anywhere yet; no human review is performed, recorded, or implied by anything here.

2. **Consumption, exact:** ADR-0038's routing and future-owned classes and V5; ADR-0039 whole; ADR-0040's decision 4 boundary and decision 10 accompaniment seats; the ledger's no-bulk-approve obligation; the W5 evaluation posture; the closure carry-forward inventories. Nothing consumed is weakened, and conflicts resolve upward through ADR-0040 into ADR-0039 into ADR-0038.

### Part B — Review-routed deltas

3. **A routed delta displays whole:** both variant captures — or both compared surfaces — exactly as the source records hold them, together with the fact of routing and the recorded reason for routing where the record carries one. The human sees what the instrument saw, in the instrument's own content-free references, and nothing less.

4. **Display never does the review.** No recommendation, no score, no verdict, no winner, no better option, no safer option, no pass/fail, no approval, no rejection — by word, ordering, emphasis, or glyph. **The two variants are never collapsed into a machine-selected conclusion, and neither variant is hidden — because there is no selected variant unless a human record later creates one.** Presenting variants symmetrically is not a style choice; asymmetry is selection wearing layout.

5. **Where no human decision exists, the state remains** ***routed to human review / awaiting human review*** — a routing label naming a destination and a status, displayed under ADR-0040 decision 4, for as long as that is the truth. Time does not escalate it, and no display ages a routed item into an implied conclusion.

6. **A future human decision displays as a human act or not at all:** attributed to human review, citing the governed source that records the act, rendered as a review label under ADR-0038 — never as system judgement, never unattributed, never summarised into a machine-looking state. **No current W6 doctrine record creates such an act**, and this record does not define the format of one; if a future governed record ever does, its display obeys this decision from birth.

### Part C — Pending stubs

7. **A pending stub displays with its whole honest identity:** the stub's identity, its owner, its dependency, and its unblocking condition, exactly as the ledger records them — and **displays as pending, not converted**, for as long as that is the truth.

8. **Display can neither convert nor invite conversion.** No convert, approve, approve-all, resolve, retire, or mark-done affordance may exist near a stub — no button, toggle, swipe, checkbox, or action copy (ADR-0038 V5, applied by name). **Eligibility arrives only through the work the stub waits on, and conversion arrives only through its own separate governed ceremony; display performs neither, implies neither, and counts down to neither.**

9. **No bulk path, by any register.** The no-bulk-approve obligation binds words, visuals, grouping, and affordance equally: no multi-select over stubs, no "clear all", no grouping that presents stubs as a batch awaiting one gesture. And **stubs are never grouped as defects, failures, or backlog shame** — a pending stub is the ledger's honesty working, not a debt — nor hidden because downstream work has made them uncomfortable: the nine stubs displayed alongside their finished siblings are the proof the ledger still tells the truth.

### Part D — Carried open questions

10. **A carried question displays as carried — still alive.** Its identity, its source, its owner or owning future record where recorded, and its carried status display together; and it **never** displays as resolved, retired, irrelevant, ignored, not applicable, deferred forever, or a roadmap promise. Carried is a state with standing: the question outlived five phases because retiring it silently was always the failure mode.

11. **Three waits, kept distinct in display:** a **carried open question** (a decision no record has yet made), a **pending stub** (a proof waiting on capability and ceremony), and a **deferred dependency** (an evidence row waiting on a named subject, per ADR-0040 decision 5) are three different truths and may not be merged into one generic "open items" pile — merging waits is how one of them quietly disappears.

12. **A question may not vanish for a prettier surface.** Where a surface displays the domain a carried question lives in, the question displays with it; omission is the V4 concealment overclaim, applied here by name.

### Part E — Human attribution

13. **The attribution law, complete:** a human decision may display **only** if a governed source records a human act · the display attributes it as human, never as machine, and cites its source · **no current W6 doctrine record creates such a human act** · **human-review-needed is not human-reviewed** · **routed-to-review is not reviewed** · and **reviewed is not approved** — approval may display only where the recorded human act itself says approval **and** a future governed record authorises displaying that act. Every link in this chain is a distinct state, and display may never walk the chain on its own.

### Part F — Visual semantics for routed, pending, and carried material

14. **Barred forms, consuming V1–V6 for this record's states:** no verdict glyphs on routed, pending, or carried items · **no urgency theatre** — no pulsing, badging, counting, or alarm-styling unless urgency is a recorded source state and a future law permits its display · no red-as-failure on pending, carried, or review-routed states · no green-as-cleared anywhere near them · **no progress bars or meters that imply pending work is closer to completion** — a wait is not a percentage · no collapse or filter default that hides stubs, deltas, or carried questions while showing their siblings · and no affordance of any kind that implies conversion, approval, retirement, or resolution without ceremony. Visual semantics obey every textual rule in this record exactly.

### Part G — The accompaniments

15. **Five accompaniments, non-authority labels under ADR-0038, travelling in reach and unabbreviated:** review-routed delta → *routed to human review; no machine judgement exists or is implied* · pending stub → *pending stub; conversion requires separate governed ceremony* · carried open question → *carried open question; still alive, not retired* · human-attributed act → *human act recorded; display preserves attribution and source* · where no act exists → *no human decision recorded*. These fill the seats ADR-0040 decision 10 reserved, completing the accompaniment table across all governed display states.

### Part H — Boundaries

16. **What later records and artefacts consume from this record:** DR-W6-05's catalogue validation consumes the accompaniments and barred forms as string-set and declaration obligations; DR-W6-06's surface contract consumes decisions 3–15 as the routed-material conformance set; the W6-D4 era's surfaces prove them under their own briefs.

17. **This record does not decide:** detailed rendering of the ADR-0040 states beyond their fixed laws · catalogue identity and the catalogue-ID namespace (DR-W6-05) · any catalogue artefact or final catalogue ID · any string grading · any rendered label · the review-surface boundary contract (DR-W6-06) · any UI or surface implementation · **any actual human review workflow, any approval or conversion ceremony, and any human decision record format** — the last named only as a future-owned boundary in decision 6.

18. **W6-D1 remains open; W6-D2 through W6-D6 remain unopened; W7 remains unopened.** No stub converts, no question retires, no review occurs, no human act is invented, no fixture, evaluation record, Lane C row, Tier 3 row, or applicability record changes, no model is contacted, and no dependency is added. The nine stubs remain pending, the routed deltas remain routed, and every carried question remains alive.

## Alternatives considered

- **Machine-assisted triage of routed deltas (rejected firmly).** A "suggested disposition" is ADR-0039 bind (xi)'s named breach; the instrument refused to judge, and the display may not judge on its behalf.
- **A unified "open items" inbox (rejected).** Merging deltas, stubs, and questions into one pile invites clearing them with one habit; three waits stay three displays.
- **Progress indicators for pending work (rejected).** A wait rendered as a percentage is a promise nobody made; stubs wait on ceremony, not on time.
- **Auto-archiving long-carried questions (rejected, ruled).** Age is not resolution. A question carried five phases displays as five phases carried — that longevity is information, not clutter.
- **Deferring the human-attribution chain to the workflow that will need it (rejected).** The chain is exactly what a workflow would be tempted to blur; it is law before any workflow exists so no workflow can be born blurry.

## Consequences

- The reviewer's screen is now bound before it exists: whole deltas, symmetric variants, stubs with their names and owners, questions still breathing — and not one control that does the human's job.
- The no-bulk-approve obligation and the no-machine-verdict bind now have their presentation-layer law; DR-W6-06 inherits a precise conformance set.
- The accompaniment table begun in ADR-0040 is complete across every governed display state.

## Constitutional check

- **Law 1** — routing displays as invitation; nothing pushes, badges, or alarms a person into reviewing.
- **Law 3** — no ordering, emphasis, or collapse lends a variant, stub, or question authority its source lacks; selection without a human act is barred in every register.
- **Law 11** — no person is gated: display states describe artefacts, and no review state conditions anyone's rights.
- **Law 13** — attributions, sources, and accompaniments are auditable data at the artefacts that bear them.
- **W0 Non-Goal 7** — nothing routed, pending, or carried can be dressed as a certification-style claim.
- **No new authority, no new namespace** — no human-act format is created, no identifier allocated, nothing implemented.
