# 0050 — Finding-Is-An-Event and the Finding-Disposition Mechanism (W7-D2-D)

**Status:** Accepted by human reviewer, 2026-08-20. Not a build instruction. Authorises no implementation by itself.
**Date:** August 2026 · **Phase:** W7 — First-Contact Governance and Synthetic Model Evaluation · **Deliverable:** **W7-D2** (landing **W7-D2-D**, the fourth of five)
**Position:** the fourth W7-D2 landing. ADR-0049 fixed the finding event's five fields and left three things unfinished on purpose: **what `locus` is, what `finding_id` means, and what a `disposition` may say.** This record finishes exactly those three and the machinery around them. **It creates nothing:** no directory, no schema file, no manifest, no record, no proof module, no identifier, and no payload text.
**Constitutional references:** W0 Laws 1, 3, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13; W0 §10; W0 Non-Goal 7. **No law is amended.**
**North star carried:** **generated text may enter the public Wing only as synthetic, governed evaluation evidence** — never authority, advice, truth, safety evidence, approval, or a decision about any person.
**Resolves:** none.

---

**A scan finding is not a permission question. It is an event — and the whole risk of this deliverable is that "event" quietly becomes "formality", the way every allowlist in every repository began life as a careful exception. So this record has no word for forgiveness. It has a word for *not yet looked at*, a word for *with a person*, and a word for *this one decided it*. What it does not have, anywhere in its vocabulary, is a state meaning the finding stopped mattering. And the reason it does not need one is two parts machinery and one part decision: the scanner fails on an unsuppressed finding and the suite requires the scanner to pass — but **where the machinery offers an allowlist route, this record is the governed act that refuses to take it — and where it offers no such route, there is nothing to refuse.** The machine exposed an escape in one place. Governance declines to use it there, and says so in those words rather than borrowing the authority of arithmetic, or claiming credit for a door the machinery never opened.**

## Decision question

**When does a scan finding come into existence relative to capture, assembly and landing; what exactly is a `locus`; what does a `finding_id` identify; what may a `disposition` say; what makes a record non-landable; and what happens when captured text cannot be made publicly lawful without destroying it as evidence — decided now, inside ADR-0049's five-field finding event, without adding a field, creating a schema, or writing a proof?**

## Controlling law

- **ADR-0049 (W7-D2-C), whole and binding as fixed input** — the finding event is exactly `{finding_id, capture_ref, category, locus, disposition}` and **there is no sixth field**; `capture_ref` is a variant label present in the same record; `category` is bounded to the live scan's own category names; `disposition` is required on every finding and its value set is **this record's**; **`locus`'s inner shape is this record's**; **no matched-text field exists**; capture scan-status coherence (decision 60); pair completeness by key-set identity; the four structural absences; input-source non-recirculation (decision 61); the 46 schema-property positions across twelve fixed shapes, which this record may not change.
- **ADR-0048 (W7-D2-B)** — the reserved home; registered manifest per run with a hash per record; **captured text never edited once landed, terminal in time as well as flow**; the shape is not permission.
- **ADR-0046** — decision 5 **capture is publication**; decisions 8–10 the closed origin set and no-recirculation; **decisions 11–12 the closed, growable-only, never-narrowable exclusion list**; **decision 13 no exemption for machine production, a finding is a governed event with a disposition path, never silently allowlisted**; decisions 19–26 declarability and the ceiling with specimen parity.
- **ADR-0047** — decisions 12–15 one-way contact, no retroactive authorisation, **a generated byte before its gate is a stop-and-report event**, and the honest statement that doctrine cannot prevent the act it governs; decisions 16–17 no-recirculation and finding-is-an-event applied to contact.
- **`W7-AR`** — §3 north star; **§6's requirement that captures be scan-clean or governed by an explicit bounded exception**; §8.1; §9; **§10's tripwires, including *generated output that cannot be made public-safe without distortion***; §13.
- **ADR-0007** — decision 1 synthetic-only; **decision 2 paste is publication**; decision 4 no machine-identifying detail; **decision 6 no content strings in commit, review or issue text — synthetic included**.
- **ADR-0034** — A4 decisions 10–12; **B6 decision 34, records scanned exactly as documents are with no exemption for machine production**.
- **`W7-D2-RSB`** — the six standing statements; all W7-D2 proofs land in W7-D2-E.
- **The live machinery, read as code rather than as doctrine** — `scripts/public-safety-scan.py`: `tracked_files()` returns **every tracked file with no extension filter**; `scan_file` reads any UTF-8-decodable file and matches **line by line, 1-based**; a non-empty finding set returns **exit 1**; the allowlist match is **`path == rel and category == c and fingerprint in line`**. `scripts/scan-allowlist.txt`: four fields, path-scoped, **no globs**. `tests/test_repo_state.py::test_scan_normal_mode_passes` asserts the normal scan **returns 0**.
- **The corrected W7-D2 combined design packet, Component D — planning history only and not authority.** ADR-0049 supersedes its proposed finding shape.

## Decisions

### Part A — The door

1. **This record decides a mechanism and builds none of it.** After its landing the repository contains exactly one new governed artefact: this record. **`governance/generated-evaluation/` remains absent. No schema file exists. No proof module exists. No manifest exists. No GER record exists. No GER identifier is allocated.** No run, probe, fixture, harness, adapter or dependency. **No generated-output, authored-specimen, capture-payload or example-payload text is authored, generated or imported by this landing.**

2. **D does not discharge W7-D2 by itself.** W7-D2-E remains unlanded. **W7-D3 remains unbegun and the model boundary remains unchosen. No model may be contacted after this record lands.**

3. **ADR-0049 is fixed input and is not reopened.** This record adds no finding field, changes no schema position, no nullable position, no pair or capture shape, no `scan_status` value, no ceiling, no synthetic marker, no digest rule and no identity law. **The packet's Component D is planning history; where it and ADR-0049 differ, ADR-0049 governs and the packet is discarded.**

### Part B — Finding-is-an-event: the order of operations

4. **A finding comes into existence at capture-time scan, before the capture is written into an assembling record, and long before anything is landed.** The sequence is fixed and not reorderable:

| # | Step | What exists afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Capture** — text arrives and is held in the run's working state | a capture payload, unwritten |
| 2 | **Capture-time scan** — the scan runs against the decoded capture text | zero or more findings, each `open` |
| 3 | **Assembly** — the record is composed, findings included, `scan_status` set per capture | a candidate record, unlanded |
| 4 | **Pre-landing review** — a human dispositions every finding | every finding in a terminal or holding state |
| 5 | **Landing predicate** — Part L is evaluated | either a landable record, or a stop-and-report |
| 6 | **Commit** — only a landable record reaches this step | published bytes |

5. **The check belongs at capture time because capture is publication.** ADR-0007 decision 2 binds the act that moves text, not the act that publishes a file. **Upstream scanning discharges nothing:** that a probe, a fixture, or an earlier artefact scanned clean says nothing about what came back, and **no cleanliness anywhere upstream transfers to a capture.** A capture that cannot be scanned at capture time may not be made.

6. **The event is created by the scan, not by a person.** A human dispositions a finding; **no human creates or deletes one.** There is no act, at any step, that removes a finding from a record while retaining the capture that produced it — deletion of a finding is deletion of evidence, and this record provides no path to it.

### Part C — Finding identity

7. **`finding_id` is record-local and meaning-free, and this record mints no namespace.** Finding identity is the pair **`(record_id, finding_id)`**; `finding_id` alone identifies nothing outside its record. The form is a short local label of the shape `F` followed by digits.

8. **Why not a governed namespace.** ADR-0049 minted `GER-####` because a record needs identity across the repository; **a finding does not.** A finding exists only inside one record, is referenced only from inside it, and never outsurvives it. **A repository-wide finding namespace would create an identifier that could be cited, counted, ranked and tracked across runs** — which is four kinds of authority a finding must never acquire. **This record therefore mints nothing and says so plainly rather than creating a namespace quietly.**

9. **Allocation order carries nothing.** `F2` is not later, larger, worse, more severe, more urgent, more reviewed or more important than `F1`. It is a label distinguishing one finding from another **inside a single record and nowhere else.**

### Part D — `locus`: the owned inner shape

10. **`locus` is an object with exactly one field, an integer, and no string member is permitted at any depth.**

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `character_start` | integer, **1-based** | the position, counted in Unicode code points of the **decoded capture `text`**, at which the match begins |

11. **The coordinate system, fixed exactly, with no union anywhere.** **One coordinate, 1-based, counted in Unicode code points of the decoded `text` value** — not UTF-8 bytes, not grapheme clusters, not lines. **Match-start only: there is no end coordinate, no length, and no range.** **No normalisation of any kind** is applied before counting, matching ADR-0049's rule that the capture is preserved as received.

12. **The design was re-derived after a line-based coordinate was assessed and rejected on live behaviour.** A `{line, ordinal}` locus was drafted and discarded for two reasons found in code rather than argued: **Python's `str.splitlines()` splits on far more than U+000A** — a decoded capture containing U+2028, U+000C or U+0085 yields **four segments under `splitlines()` where splitting on U+000A alone yields one** — so any line coordinate would have had to either diverge from the scanner's own line arithmetic or inherit boundaries no line-ending rule in this repository names. And a category-relative ordinal **would have depended on future regex and category-matching behaviour staying constant**, making the coordinate a hostage to the scanner rather than a fact about the capture. **A single code-point offset into the decoded string depends on nothing but the string.**

13. **Reconciled with ADR-0049's digest rule, explicitly.** `text_digest` hashes **the UTF-8 bytes** of the decoded string; `locus` identifies **a decoded-string code-point position within that same value.** Both derive from `text` and neither derives from the file, so a change in serialisation, escaping or line endings moves neither. **The locus is deliberately not a byte offset**, because a byte position over a value whose digest is taken in bytes would look like the same coordinate while being encoding-dependent.

14. **Independence, stated as the property it is.** `character_start` is **independent of JSON serialisation**, of escaping, of file layout, of line-ending convention, and **of future regex or category behaviour**. It is **mechanically resolvable** against the capture present under `capture_ref` in the same record, and resolvable by anyone holding that capture and nothing else.

15. **`locus` cannot become a text-bearing channel, structurally.** **One integer field, no string member at any depth, no additional key.** A number cannot carry a phrase, a fingerprint, an excerpt or a paraphrase. **This is the second place in the schema where the absence of a field, rather than a rule about its use, is the enforcement** — the first being ADR-0049's missing matched-text field. **What a locus does not carry:** no matched text · no fingerprint · no excerpt · no paraphrase · no length · no end position · no severity · no confidence · no count · no cross-reference.

16. **One seam is reported here rather than solved: `local-wordlist` semantics do not guarantee a lawful `character_start` for every finding they can emit.** **The scanner returns no start coordinate for any finding today.** For the committed public patterns that is a **derivation** gap rather than a semantic one — and the derivation is not trivial, because the live scanner calls **`pattern.finditer(line)`** where `line` came from `text.splitlines()`, so **`m.start()` is an offset into the scanned line, not already a full-capture offset.** The match start is **unambiguous within that line**, and a later governed implementation can derive the required full-capture `character_start` by **retaining or deriving that line's base position in the original decoded text and adding the line-relative match start.** **This record does not prescribe how.**

   **The `local-wordlist` branch is harder, for two independent reasons.** First, **occurrence ambiguity**: the branch emits **one finding when `term.lower() in line.lower()` is true**, and **it neither selects nor retains an occurrence** — so where the same term appears more than once on a line, **the existing semantics do not say which occurrence the single emitted finding refers to.** Second, **mapping**: **Unicode lowercasing can expand characters**, so a position in the lowered value **does not universally correspond one-to-one with the code-point boundary at which that same match begins in the original capture.** **Unicode expansion is a second problem, not the only one.** **The current `local-wordlist` semantics therefore do not guarantee a uniquely lawful original-text `character_start` for every possible emitted finding.**

   **This record states the requirement and the seam, and pre-decides neither the fix nor its location.** The requirement: **every finding carries a `character_start` resolvable against the capture in the same record.** The seam: **before a `local-wordlist` finding can satisfy that law, a later governed act must define and prove how the coordinate is produced** — which **could lawfully be owned by a change to the scanner, or by a governed capture-time resolver, or by a mechanism not yet designed.** **This record authorises none of them and chooses between none of them.** Until one exists, **a `local-wordlist` finding cannot satisfy the locus law**, which under Part L means the record does not land — the same outcome as every other finding, reached honestly rather than by pretending a coordinate exists. **No local term appears in this record, in any finding, or in any public artefact**, and none may.

### Part E — Two mechanical facts and one governed refusal

17. **Facts about the machinery, and one governed refusal. Together they make a finding-bearing record non-landable — and the refusal is a decision, not arithmetic.**

   **The machinery, verified in code:** **`tracked_files()` has no extension filter**, so a tracked record under the reserved home **is scanned like every other tracked file**; and **an unsuppressed finding makes the scanner return 1** while **`test_scan_normal_mode_passes` requires it to return 0** — so a landed record carrying an unsuppressed match makes the suite red **on every future run**.

   **The machinery supplies a way out for some findings and not for others, and the difference is in the code.** In `scan_file`, the **committed-pattern and fixture checks consult the allowlist before a finding is appended**, under the condition **`path == rel and category == c and fingerprint in line`** — **a technical suppression route** that would make such a record land and the suite pass. **The `local-wordlist` branch does not consult the allowlist at all**: when its containment test succeeds the finding is appended directly, and the scanner's own comment says so — *never allowlistable*.

   **So the universal rule holds for two different reasons, and this record names both rather than flattening them.** For a **committed-pattern finding**, the machinery offers a suppression route and **Part I is this record refusing it** — **a governed act, not a mechanical impossibility**. For a **`local-wordlist` finding**, **the live machinery already provides no such route**, so there is nothing for governance to refuse. **In both cases an unsuppressed finding makes the normal scanner return 1 while the deterministic suite requires 0.**

   **The constitutional point is preserved exactly where it applies:** **governance is what closes the allowlist escape where that escape actually exists.** This record does not borrow the authority of arithmetic for the decision it made, and does not claim credit for closing a door the machinery never opened.

18. **Therefore, under this record's law, no finding-bearing record can land, and no disposition can change that** — including a finding in the terminal `withheld_from_publication` state, since every value in Part F is non-landable. **The supporting facts are found in the machinery; the refusal of the suppression route, where one exists, is this record's own decision, and is stated as one.** The honest response is to build no mechanism that pretends the refusal is anything else, and to record the consequence where it can be seen (Part Q) rather than routing around it.

19. **What follows for the vocabulary.** Every disposition value in Part F is **non-landable**. **There is no landable finding disposition, and this record declines to invent one** — an invented landable value would be a door built beside a gate this record has just decided to keep shut.

### Part F — The disposition vocabulary

20. **Three values, closed, and none of them means the finding stopped mattering.**

| Value | Exact meaning | Terminal or holding | May a record with it land | Human judgement required | What changes the state | Captured bytes | Why it cannot mean "never mind" |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`open`** | The scan created this finding and no person has yet considered it. | **Holding** | **No** | Not yet | Routing it to a reviewer | present in the run's working state, unpublished | It asserts nothing at all. It is the **absence** of judgement, never its conclusion, and a run that stops here has stopped **before** anyone decided anything |
| **`routed_for_public_safety_review`** | The finding is before a named human for the **pre-landing public-safety** decision. | **Holding** | **No** | Yes, outstanding | The reviewer's act | present, unpublished | It names an **obligation not yet discharged**. A record cannot land while an obligation is outstanding, so the state cannot be used to ship anything |
| **`withheld_from_publication`** | A human considered the finding and **the capture and its record are not published.** | **Terminal** | **No** | Yes, made | Nothing — terminal | **not published**; they remain only in the run's working state and this repository retains them nowhere | It records that the finding was **decisive**. It is the strongest thing the vocabulary can say, and it is the opposite of forgiveness |

21. **No value may be added that means accepted, allowlisted, suppressed, waived, ignored, false-positive, safe, passed, approved, harmless — or any synonym carrying the same collapse.** **The three-value set is closed by this record.** No fourth value is justified by the mechanism this record is deciding, and **any later extension requires its own governed amendment.** **A hypothetical fourth value would not necessarily be landable** — it could be another holding or terminal state, merely redundant rather than unlawful — so closure rests on sufficiency, not on logical necessity. **What is barred outright is narrower and firmer: no later value may create a landable finding state unless the Part Q publication seam has first been resolved through its own authority.**

22. **`findings` is expressible and never landable.** ADR-0049 requires `disposition` on every finding and gives its value set to this record; this record makes every value non-landable. **The consequence is that every landed record has `findings: []` and every capture in it has `scan_status: no_findings`.** The array is not decoration: it is how a finding is **represented during assembly and carried into the stop-and-report**, which is the only place its content-free facts survive.

### Part G — Rulings on the four packet tokens

23. **`capture_excluded` — removed, because it is unlawful under ADR-0049.** Pair completeness requires `captures`' key set to equal `pairing.variant_labels` exactly. **Excluding one capture breaks the bijection**, and a disposition that produced a lawful one-capture record would be a `paired` boolean by another name — precisely the branch ADR-0049 decision 22 refused to create. **There is no lawful meaning for this token that does not create a half-pair, and its only lawful meaning collapses into not landing the record at all.**

24. **`record_not_landed` — removed, because it is not a disposition.** A disposition is **a value inside a record**. If the record does not land, the value is never published, and **a state that exists only in artefacts that do not exist is not a state of anything.** The fact that a run stopped is preserved by **the stop-and-report act of Part M**, which is a governed event and not a field. **This record does not pretend an unlanded record is durable evidence of its own non-existence.**

25. **`capture_bounded_by_named_exception` — removed, because it cannot be named inside five fields.** A governed exception must be **created by an authority, named somewhere, resolvable, and auditable**. ADR-0049 provides no `disposition_basis` and no `disposition_record`, and this record may not add a sixth field. **An exception whose name has nowhere to live is an unnamed exception, and an unnamed exception is an allowlist with better manners.** Removed rather than preserved for the appearance of nuance.

26. **A named exception could never have reached the exclusion list in any case.** ADR-0046 decisions 11–12 make that list **closed, growable-only and never narrowable**. **Anything on it is non-dispositionable for landing, full stop** — no value in Part F, and no value any future record adds, may bound, waive, scope or except it.

27. **`routed_to_human_review` — survives, renamed and narrowed to `routed_for_public_safety_review`.** The rename is the whole point: **the packet's name invited exactly the confusion Part N forbids.** It is a **holding** state, not a final disposition, and a record may not land while any finding sits in it.

### Part H — Scan finding is not exclusion-list breach

28. **Two different events, and this record refuses to collapse them.** A **scan finding** is any finding **emitted by the active scanner** against a capture — whether from **the committed public pattern set** or from **`local-wordlist` where one is configured** (decision 31). An **exclusion-list breach** is the presence of something on ADR-0046 decision 11's closed list. **They overlap in places and are not the same class**, and only one of them enters a disposition mechanism at all.

29. **The invariant, absolute:** **`exclusion_check.result == "listed_item_present"` makes the record non-landable, and no disposition overrides it.** Not `open`, not routed, not any value a later record adds. **This is not a finding to be handled; it is a fence that was already up.**

30. **A scan finding outside the closed exclusion list enters the Part F mechanism** — where, per Part E, every path is non-landable anyway. **The distinction still matters and is worth stating**, because the two events reach that same outcome for entirely different reasons, and a future record that ever makes a finding landable must not thereby make a breach landable.

31. **`category` is bounded to the committed public scan-category set, together with the scanner's `local-wordlist` category where a local wordlist is configured** — the names the scanner actually emits, exactly as ADR-0049 fixed it, and cross-checkable against the machinery.

   **This record enumerates none of them.** Two of the committed public categories are themselves scan-sensitive strings, so a record listing them would trip the scan it describes — the same self-reference ADR-0038's stem convention exists to handle. **And no local wordlist term is named here, in any finding, or in any public artefact**, which is the whole point of that category being local.

### Part I — The allowlist bar

32. **`scripts/scan-allowlist.txt` may never contain an entry whose path lies under `governance/generated-evaluation/`.** **No exact path. No future GER path. No wildcard. No glob. No category-specific workaround. No fingerprint exception. No entry of any kind.**

33. **The reason is the live mechanism's granularity, stated precisely.** **The allowlist is line-scoped, not match-scoped.** Once `path`, `category` and **any fingerprint occurring somewhere on the containing line** agree, **a match of that category on that line is suppressed — without anything proving that the fingerprint identifies the particular matched substring being excepted.**

   **That granularity is acceptable for specifically reviewed, human-authored enumerable prose**, where a person chose the path, read the line, and wrote a public-safe justification for a passage they can hold in mind. **It is too coarse for generated or specimen capture evidence**, which cannot be enumerated in advance and which no one authored: a line-scoped suppression over such a capture would let a category be quieted on a line whose contents were never specifically reviewed as that match. **That is exactly the "considered, and fine" escape this record exists to prevent** — and it is why the prohibition is absolute rather than conditional.

   **The argument does not rest on future mutation.** ADR-0048's capture immutability means a landed capture never changes, so no drift argument is needed and none is offered.

### Part J — The no-quotation law

34. **A finding event may identify its own id, its capture, its category, its structural locus, and its disposition. That is the whole of what it may carry, and ADR-0049 has already made it impossible for it to carry more.**

35. **The matched payload text may not be copied into any of:** the finding · a review packet · a commit message · a board row · a registry role · a candidate report · an issue, note or message. **Identifiers, category, coordinates and disposition only** — ADR-0007 decision 6 binds here unchanged, **synthetic included**, because content strings in commentary train reviewers to stop checking.

36. **`locus` is checked against this law as a channel and not merely as a field.** One integer, no string at any depth, no additional key: **there is no member in which a phrase could travel.** Part O's U3 makes that mechanical.

### Part K — Coherence

37. **ADR-0049 decision 60 is consumed unchanged and is not restated as new law.** For each capture: `no_findings` if and only if no finding references it; `findings_present` if and only if at least one does. **This record neither weakens, reinterprets nor duplicates it.**

38. **One coherence rule this record adds, stated separately so it is visibly D's and not C's:** **every finding's `capture_ref` must be a key present in `captures`.** A finding against a capture that is not in the record has no subject, and its locus resolves against nothing.

39. **Finding routing and evaluation routing are unrelated, and neither implies the other.** `routed_for_public_safety_review` **does not require, imply, set or read `human_review.routed`**, and `human_review.routed == true` implies nothing about any finding. **They are different reviews at different times about different questions** — Part N. In a landed record the question never arises at all, since Part L forbids landing with any finding.

### Part L — The landing predicate

40. **A record is non-landable if any of the following holds. The list is re-derived from current landed law, not carried from the packet.**

| # | Condition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | An input declares no origin, or an origin outside the closed three-value set | ADR-0046 d7–8; ADR-0049 |
| 2 | An input citation or reference resolves into the generated-evaluation class | **ADR-0049 d61** |
| 3 | `exclusion_check.result == "listed_item_present"` | **ADR-0046 d11–12; Part H, absolute** |
| 4 | `exclusion_check` absent, or `checked` not literally true | ADR-0049 |
| 5 | `synthetic_marker.synthetic` not literally `true`, or `notice` not byte-identical | ADR-0049 |
| 6 | `captures`' key set is not identical to `pairing.variant_labels` | ADR-0049 pair completeness |
| 7 | A capture declares `generated_output` while `model_contact.occurred` is false | ADR-0049 provenance constraints |
| 8 | A capture declares `authored_synthetic_specimen` with a null `authoring_record`, or `generated_output` with a non-null one | ADR-0049 |
| 9 | `non_authority` absent, altered, or not the final field | ADR-0046 d23; ADR-0049 |
| 10 | A capture's `scan_status` disagrees with its finding references | ADR-0049 d60 |
| 11 | **`findings` is non-empty** — every disposition in Part F is non-landable | **Part E; Part F** |
| 12 | A finding carries a disposition outside Part F's closed set | Part F |
| 13 | A finding's `capture_ref` is not a key in `captures` | **Part K** |
| 14 | A `locus` is malformed — not exactly one integer field `character_start`, a non-positive value, a string member, or a coordinate beyond the referenced capture's code-point length | **Part D** |
| 15 | An allowlist entry exists whose path lies under the reserved home | **Part I** |
| 16 | `model_contact.occurred` is true with a null `authorising_record`, or a `contact_class` W7-D3 has not authorised | ADR-0049 |
| 17 | `delta.outcome == "routed-to-review"` while `human_review.routed` is false | ADR-0049 d59 |
| 18 | `human_review.disposition` is non-null before W7-D6 exists, or without a `disposition_record` | ADR-0049 |
| 19 | `text_digest` does not match its `text` under ADR-0049's byte rule | ADR-0049 |

41. **Condition 11 subsumes conditions 12, 13 and 14 in practice and they are stated anyway**, because a future record that ever resolves the Part Q seam would remove 11 and must not thereby lose the others. **A predicate that collapses when its strongest clause is lifted is a predicate that was resting on one clause.**

### Part M — Stop-and-report

42. **Where a capture cannot be made publicly lawful without destroying it as evidence, the lawful outcome is that the record does not land.** Stated plainly so no later deliverable has to infer it.

43. **The following are forbidden, without exception:** editing the capture to pass the scan · removing words from captured text · paraphrasing it · replacing it with a summary · truncating it · silently dropping the finding · **and, above all, calling any altered text the original capture.** ADR-0048 decisions 22–25 make captured text terminal in time; **a capture edited to pass a check is not a capture, and a record containing one is a false account of what was produced.**

44. **The minimum content-free facts the reporting act preserves:** the record identifier if one was allocated by the run · the run identifier · the affected capture's variant label · the finding's category · its locus · its disposition · and the outcome that the record did not land. **Never the matched text, never a fingerprint, never an excerpt, never a paraphrase, and never a count that could only have been derived from reading the payload aloud.**

45. **This record does not create the report artefact and does not own it.** The reporting form belongs to **W7-D5**, the first deliverable that will have runs to report on; **this record fixes only the content-free minimum any such report must carry and the acts it may never perform.** Naming the owner is deliberate: silence would read as an assignment to nobody.

### Part N — Anti-collapse

46. **The keystone rule, stated as its own decision because it is the one most likely to erode:** **a finding disposition is not a human-review disposition.** The first governs **whether and how a scan finding is handled before public retention**; the second is **W7-D6's later human judgement about the evaluation record itself**. **Neither may stand in for the other, be read from the other, or be written by the act that writes the other.** Nothing in this record defines, constrains or pre-decides W7-D6's vocabulary.

47. **The chain, normative and non-collapsible:** **finding ≠ verdict · finding ≠ winner · routing ≠ approval · bounded ≠ safe · no finding ≠ safety · scan clean ≠ public-safe in substance · disposition ≠ judgement about the evaluation · unlanded ≠ unrecorded · terminal ≠ resolved.**

48. **This record does not move W7-D6 earlier in the phase.** A later GER may lawfully exist with `human_review.disposition` and `disposition_record` both null — that is already ADR-0049's law — and **the pre-landing public-safety review of Part F is a different act by a different reviewer answering a different question.** Finding handling makes W7-D6 no earlier a prerequisite than it was.

## Part Q — The unresolved seam, reported rather than routed around

53. **ADR-0046 decision 13 contemplates two outcomes for a finding: *"the record does not land, or it lands with the finding recorded and the text bounded or excluded."* If this record lands, the second outcome has no implementation under the resulting law.** ADR-0049's five-field shape removed the fields in which a bounding exception could be named; pair completeness removed exclusion of a capture; and, under Part E, **a committed-pattern finding would remain unsuppressed because this record refuses the available allowlist route, while a `local-wordlist` finding already has no such route in the live machinery.** **Under the resulting law, either kind of finding makes the GER non-landable.**

54. **The consequence is real and narrowing, and it is stated rather than softened.** **Under this record's proposed law, any capture that produces a scan finding under the active scanner configuration cannot be published as a GER** — including patterns a model is comparatively likely to produce, and which the evaluation exists in order to observe. **A model making the kind of claim the scan bars is precisely an interesting finding, and this repository currently has no lawful way to publish the evidence of it.**

55. **This record does not resolve that, and should not.** The two candidate resolutions are **a governed change to how the scan treats this class** — which ADR-0034 decision 34's *no exemption for machine production* presently forbids — or **accepting the narrowing** and recording what W7 therefore cannot demonstrate. **Both are decisions for the human authority and the architect, not for a mechanism record**, and either would need its own ceremony. **Until then the mechanism is the strict one, and the limitation is visible rather than engineered around.**

## Consequences

**Made easier:** the finding mechanism has exactly one shape, one coordinate system, and three words; W7-D2-E writes predicates rather than interpreting intent; and a reviewer meeting a finding has no state available to them that could be mistaken for approval.

**Made harder, deliberately:** a run that produces a finding produces no published record, so an interesting failure may cost the evidence of itself. Every finding must be dispositioned by a person before anything lands. And no exception exists to be argued for at the moment one would be most persuasive.

**Accepted cost, in three kinds.** **One obligation can never be mechanised** — U6, whether a human's public-safety judgement was right, joins the five review-only duties already standing. **Four are debts** against a class that does not exist. **And one limitation is architectural rather than technical:** Part Q's seam narrows what public W7 can show, and this record leaves it visible for a decision it does not have the authority to make.
