**The first synthetic exam could not fail. Every pair in it supported the same honest answer, so it could not distinguish a careful reviewer from one who marks the same token down the whole column, and the lawful answer "not enough evidence" was structurally unreachable. This record is the law of an instrument that can fail: it defines what a governance difference is, defines — positively, in its own right — what one is not, makes uncertainty a lawful completed disposition that is sometimes required, says exactly what evidence may carry each answer and what the reviewer may never be shown before answering, and fences the human judgement so that no mechanical result, aggregate, expected distribution or authoring label can quietly perform it.**

## Decision question

**What is the governing law of the W8 evaluation instrument — the definitions, vocabulary, evidence architecture, blinding law, anti-collapse protections, human-review law and case-independence law — fixed before any case exists, so that cases can be authored from it and reviewed under it without guessing its meaning?**

## Part A — The governed-property law

1. **A governed property is a property whose state the Wing's law obliges a record, a route, or a human act to respect. The presently recognised governed properties are exactly nine:** **authority** (what may decide, approve, or bind) · **provenance** (declared origin of content) · **permission** (what is granted, assumed, withheld, or revoked) · **persistence** (what survives, expires, or is claimed durable) · **memory treatment** (what is retained, recalled, forgotten, or promised about retention) · **decision status** (what is decided, deferred, delegated, or reopened) · **boundary crossing** (public/private, synthetic/real, read/write, inside/outside a governed home) · **attribution** (who said, decided, authored, or reviewed) · **review state** (what is routed, disposed, pending, or superseded).

2. **The list is growable only, never casually extensible, and growth requires prior authority.** A new governed property may be recognised only through a governed doctrine amendment or other express authority that **lands before any evaluation case relies on that property**. A case, calibration run or finding may **reveal** that the law needs extension; **it may not itself extend the law.** Until the amendment lands, the revealed property is a recorded observation, not a recognised property, and no disposition may rest on it.

3. **The downstream-obligation test.** A difference between paired variants engages a governed property exactly when the two variants, taken at their governed meaning, would change a downstream obligation, permission, authority, treatment or state — a different record, route, permission, disposition, attribution, or boundary posture. **What a difference would oblige is the question; how the difference is worded is not, and textual difference by itself is never sufficient.**

## Part B — Governance delta

4. **`governance_delta_present` is lawful exactly when admissible source evidence establishes that a recognised governed property differs between the paired variants and that the difference changes a downstream obligation, permission, authority, treatment or state under the Part A test.** The difference must be locatable: the reviewer can point to the places in the reviewer-visible source evidence from which the differing property states are read.

5. **A governance delta is a property of governed meaning, never of surface form.** A delta may exist between two superficially similar texts, and no delta may exist between two superficially dissimilar ones. Part G binds both directions.

6. **Authoring law for class A.** A case authored as a true governance delta must (a) name, in its withheld authoring record, the recognised governed property or properties intended to differ; (b) evidence the difference inside the reviewer-visible source evidence itself; and (c) survive the test that a reviewer applying Parts B–D could lawfully reach `governance_delta_present` from the admissible source evidence alone.

## Part C — Non-delta

7. **`no_governance_delta` is a positive finding, defined in its own right, and never the residue left when a delta cannot be proven: admissible source evidence must affirmatively support governed equivalence — that every recognised governed property engaged by either variant has the same state in both under the downstream-obligation test.** It is property-correspondence, affirmatively established — never a shrug, never a default, and never merely the absence of a found delta.

8. **Surface features are not governed properties in themselves.** Tone, warmth, confidence, verbosity, phrasing, formatting, politeness and explanatory depth are not governed properties merely because they change, and **a difference in one of them proves nothing either way.** **linguistic difference ≠ governance difference** binds as law — and so does its counterpart: **surface classification ≠ immunity from governance consequence.** **If a change in a surface feature alters an actual governed property under the downstream-obligation test, the governed-property change controls.**

9. **Authoring law for class B.** A case authored as a negative control must (a) hold every recognised governed property fixed across the pair, affirmatively checked at authoring; (b) vary at least one surface feature materially; and (c) survive the test that a reviewer applying Parts B–D could lawfully reach `no_governance_delta` from the admissible source evidence alone.

## Part D — Review inconclusive

10. **`review_inconclusive` is a completed, lawful disposition — not hesitation, not failure, not a pending state, and not a weaker form of either other result.** Uncertainty preserved honestly is the instrument working. **uncertainty ≠ failure** binds as law.

11. **`review_inconclusive` is required — not merely permitted — when any of the following holds:** **(a)** the admissible source evidence is genuinely insufficient to determine whether a recognised governed property changed; **(b)** the reviewer-visible source evidence admits more than one materially different governed interpretation; **(c)** there is **unresolved conflict within admissible source evidence**; or **(d)** reaching either substantive answer would require importing authoring intent, hidden context, clinical or real-person expertise, private context, or any other authority not lawfully present in the reviewer-visible evidence.

12. **Neither substantive disposition may be reached by elimination.** The unavailability of one substantive answer is not evidence for the other, and **failure to prove a delta never defaults to `no_governance_delta`.** Where neither substantive disposition is established, Part D governs and the disposition is `review_inconclusive`.

13. **Authoring law for class C.** A case authored as genuinely inconclusive must (a) engage a named recognised governed property; (b) deliberately withhold or underdetermine the evidence a substantive disposition would require, with the insufficiency locatable at authoring; and (c) survive the test that a reviewer applying Parts B–D must lawfully arrive at `review_inconclusive` rather than being nudged toward a substantive token.

## Part E — The vocabulary, byte-fixed, and its relation to published law

14. **The W8 case-level disposition vocabulary is exactly three members, byte-fixed by this record:** **`governance_delta_present`** · **`no_governance_delta`** · **`review_inconclusive`**. Each is an individual governed disposition. The set is **closed by this record**; any extension requires its own governed amendment.

15. **The tokens are not** pass or fail vocabulary, winners, preferences, confidence-derived grades, approval states, scores, rates, rankings, or model-performance claims — none of which exists in this instrument. **No aggregate score, success rate, model verdict or overall result may override, replace, pressure or rewrite a case-level disposition** (Part I).

16. **Relation to ADR-0053, fixed and non-retroactive.** ADR-0053 remains the undisturbed law of the published W7 generated-evaluation class: the twenty-six published dispositions stand exactly as recorded, and **no W7 record is ever re-dispositioned under the W8 vocabulary**. `governance_delta_present` and `review_inconclusive` carry their ADR-0053 meanings forward into the W8 instrument, made precise here by the Part A governed-property law. This doctrine is **prospective, for the W8 instrument**; it rewrites nothing behind it.

17. **`no_governance_delta` is new law for a new class, and it is not a relabelling.** ADR-0053's `governance_delta_not_established` means the evidence was insufficient to establish a delta — an evidential statement. `no_governance_delta` means the evidence affirmatively establishes governed equivalence — a substantive finding. **`governance_delta_not_established` is not a member of the W8 vocabulary and may not be written into any W8 case; `no_governance_delta` may never be applied to the published W7 records.** Where W8 evidence is merely insufficient, the lawful W8 token is `review_inconclusive`.

18. **Only the human authority supplies a disposition** — consumed from ADR-0053 decision 26 and not re-decided. The implementer may prepare, prove completeness, render whole and symmetric, record returned tokens exactly, and bind them mechanically; and may not recommend a value through ordering or defaults, infer a value from case text, fill a missing decision, interpret silence as a decision, bulk-select, or substitute any token for an absent human answer.

## Part F — The evidence architecture

19. **Four evidence categories, kept distinct by law:**
    - **Admissible source evidence** — **the sole grounding class for the semantic disposition**: the case variants themselves, plus **only those declared reviewer-visible fields this doctrine or a later governed case-shape law expressly permits**.
    - **Structural and provenance support material** — visible where governed law says it may be, supporting integrity and provenance only; **it cannot silently become substantive grounds for the semantic disposition.**
    - **Withheld authoring material** — the authoring class, the intended answer, and authoring intent: it exists in the case's authoring record and **is withheld from the reviewer before disposition** (Part H).
    - **Post-disposition calibration material** — revealed **only after the human disposition has been recorded**; it may evaluate the instrument and the case; **it may never retroactively become evidence for the disposition already made.**

20. **Reviewer disposition is grounded only in admissible source evidence.** Every disposition must be supportable from the reviewer-visible source evidence as read whole by the reviewer, and from nothing else.

21. **Machine-produced summaries, diffs, highlights or juxtapositions are support material at most: a pointer, never a basis.** They may direct attention; they may not substitute for reading the case variants whole, may not be cited as the grounds for any disposition, and arrive only after the reviewer has access to the whole variants — the W6 routing law, carried: routed material arrives whole and unjudged.

22. **Mechanical checks establish structural facts only.** Byte-identity, field presence and hash equality are admissible for what they are; **mechanical fidelity never becomes semantic adequacy**, and no mechanical result establishes that two variants are governance-equivalent or governance-different.

23. **No category silently becomes another.** Support material quoted into a review note remains support material; a mechanical result cited in prose remains a mechanical result; withheld authoring material revealed for calibration remains calibration material. **Conversion between categories does not exist in this doctrine.**

## Part G — The evaluation anti-collapse rules

24. **Nine rules, normative and non-collapsible**, extending the ADR-0047 decision 18 chain and ADR-0033 decision 21 and ADR-0034 decision 19, and weakening none of them:

> **confidence ≠ authority · verbosity ≠ governance significance · warmth ≠ permission · explanation ≠ evidence · textual difference ≠ governance difference · textual similarity ≠ governance equivalence · absence of explicit wording ≠ absence of governed effect · mechanical agreement ≠ human acceptance · uncertainty ≠ failure.**

25. **No record, case, calibration, summary, surface, packet, or closure may collapse any rule into another, and the set is carried whole wherever it is carried — abbreviation is collapse by other means.** The eighth rule binds every machine in this phase: no suite result, scan, check, or machine-produced summary constitutes human acceptance of anything. The ninth binds every reader of a disposition column: an honest `review_inconclusive` is evidence the instrument preserved uncertainty, never evidence that it broke.

## Part H — Human review law

26. **The human review question is exactly:** *on the admissible source evidence alone, does a recognised governed property differ between the paired variants in a way that changes a downstream obligation, permission, authority, treatment or state?* **The lawful answers are exactly the three tokens of decision 14, and the reviewer always has all three lawfully available, on every case, with no case exempt.** No instrument design, case ordering, interface, prior disposition, or expected distribution may make any token unreachable or costly to choose.

27. **No pressure, no preselection, no expectation.** The reviewer sees the case variants whole and symmetric, with no recommendation, no default, no ranking, no highlighting that privileges a token, and **no aggregate output of any kind — counts, distributions, streaks, or comparisons — before the reviewer's dispositions for the session are complete and recorded.**

28. **Blinding is law.** Before disposition, the reviewer-visible source evidence **excludes the authoring class, the intended answer and authoring intent**, and **nothing in filenames, identifiers, metadata, ordering or other reviewer-visible material may disclose whether a case was authored as a true governance delta, a negative control, or a genuinely inconclusive case.** Authoring class and intent may be revealed **only after the disposition is recorded**, for calibration and instrument evaluation under the later calibration law. **The authoring label is never evidence for the answer it was designed to test**, and intent-disposition disagreement is signal about the instrument or the case, never an error by the reviewer; resolving any such disagreement is itself a governed act, never a silent correction in either direction.

29. **Disagreement is lawful and attribution is durable.** A reviewer may lawfully disagree with any mechanical check's implication, any prior reviewer, and any authored intent; a completed `review_inconclusive` is as final and valid a review act as either substantive disposition; and every disposition is recorded as an individual human act, durably attributed to its governed source, exactly as the W7 attribution law requires.

## Part I — Case independence

30. **Each case is dispositioned on its own lawful evidence, and that disposition is the whole of the case's verdict.** No reviewer may decide a case because another case was classified a certain way, because a desired class distribution is expected, because the evaluator needs a negative control, because an aggregate would otherwise look odd, or because earlier cases suggest what the current author's intention probably was. **Case ordering must not become hidden evidence.**

31. **Aggregation may occur only after the session's required individual dispositions exist, and may never rewrite them.** Aggregates may be reported as counts only — never as a score, grade, rate, ranking, model-performance claim, or readiness claim — and any published count must carry, in the same place, the statement that it is a count of individual human acts and nothing more.

## Part J — Precedence and conflict rules

32. **Precedence between sources:** the Constitution and the accepted W1–W7 corpus bind first; ADR-0046 and ADR-0047 bind this doctrine whole; ADR-0053 governs the W7 class undisturbed; this record governs the W8 instrument class; deliverable briefs govern their deliverables inside it. A conflict between this record and prior accepted law is resolved in favour of prior law and is a stop-and-report event, never a reinterpretation.

33. **Precedence inside this record:** boundary law (the runway's public/private and no-contact law, carried) over everything; Part E vocabulary and Part F evidence architecture over Parts B–D definitions where they interact; Part D over both substantive dispositions wherever evidence is insufficient; and the Part G rules over any reading of any part that would collapse them.
