---

**W1-D6 wrote the sentence for tests: passing tests must not mint authority. This record writes it for pixels. A surface stands between a governed source and a human eye, and everything it adds — a word, a colour, an ordering, a silence — is either the source's authority carried faithfully, or new authority invented in transit. The firewall is the structural guarantee that only the first kind can exist, stated as one formula the rest of this record merely unpacks: display may preserve source authority; display may not create authority.**

## Decision question

**How does label language — including visual language — remain structurally unable to mint authority: by what mechanism, proven how, such that no later catalogue, grading record, or surface can show approval, confidence, correctness, safety, or completion that its governed source does not hold?**

## Controlling law

- **The accepted W6-D1 brief** — law 13 assigned here; the sequencing note making this record the second to land, consuming DR-W6-01.
- **ADR-0038, whole and unweakened** — the seven vocabulary classes, class-as-data, verbatim-or-registered-derived display, derived-never-stored, the barred display list with the `certif-` and `complian-` stem bans, the prohibited visual classes V1–V6, the declaration obligations, omission-as-overclaim, and the non-authority accompaniments. Every one is a firewall input; none is renegotiated.
- **W0 Law 3** — confidence creates no authority — and **W0 Non-Goal 7**, both already extended to display forms by ADR-0038.
- **ADR-0034** — the anti-collapse chain and §3.8's *"passing tests must not mint authority"*, translated here to presentation; **ADR-0037 decision 11** — the closed record-outcome vocabulary the firewall must carry through unimproved.
- **W4-D6-PHDAR** — the derivation rule whose output is *never a pass*: the founding example of an authority-non-increasing derived label.

## Decisions

### Part A — The formula and the door

1. **This record decides the firewall and displays nothing.** No label renders, no artefact exists, and no capability is authorised here.

2. **The firewall formula, normative:** **display may preserve source authority; display may not create authority.** Every rule in this record is an instance of the formula; every future display question is answered by it first.

3. **ADR-0038 is consumed whole as the firewall's input layer** — its closed vocabulary, class-as-data rule, verbatim-or-registered-derived path, derived-never-stored law, barred list and stem bans, visual classes V1–V6, declaration obligations, omission-as-overclaim rule, and non-authority accompaniments. **Nothing in ADR-0038 is weakened, narrowed, or excepted by this record**, and any conflict between the two is resolved in ADR-0038's favour with this record the defect.

### Part B — The mechanism, bound

4. **The firewall is a mechanism, not a posture — eleven binds, each already grounded in consumed law:** (i) the **closed display vocabulary**: a label outside the seven classes cannot exist to mint anything; (ii) **class-as-data**: every label's class is machine-readable, so misclassification is detectable rather than arguable; (iii) the **source-to-display derivation rule** of decision 5; (iv) **prohibited wording and stem checks** over every display-string set, consuming ADR-0038 decision 10 verbatim; (v) **prohibited visual declaration checks** over every declared glyph, colour-semantic, coverage and affordance inventory (V1–V5 deterministic, V6 declared-rule review); (vi) **non-authority accompaniment requirements** — the accompaniments of ADR-0038 Part F are firewall components and may never be weakened, abbreviated into icons, or moved out of reach; (vii) **honest-state coverage requirements** — a display artefact declares which state families it covers, and coverage that shows a family's comfortable members while omitting its uncomfortable ones fails as V4 concealment; (viii) **no write-back**: no derived presentation state may be stored into any governed source, ever, on any path; (ix) **no ranking or prominence without a declared non-quality rule**: every ordering over governed states is declared, deterministic, and named after a neutral property, never after quality, health, or score; (x) **no ceremony-bypassing affordance**, consuming V5 with the pending ledger's no-bulk-approve obligation; (xi) **no machine verdict, structurally**: a surface may show a verdict only where a governed source actually holds a human-authorised verdict as a recorded human act — **and no current W6 source holds one**. Machine outputs are states, observations, and routings; they are never verdicts, and the firewall makes a machine-attributed verdict unconstructable rather than merely discouraged.

5. **The derivation rule is authority-non-increasing, by law.** Only two lawful paths exist from source to display: **verbatim**, or **a registered deterministic derivation whose output never carries more certainty, more approval, or more finality than its input.** Every derivation rule is recorded in an accepted record and reviewed against this non-improvement condition before use; the Lane C derivation — whose overlay output is *applicability unresolved*, never a pass, whatever the underlying evidence says — is the founding member and the required shape. A derivation that improves its input is not a derivation; it is authorship of authority, and it is barred.

### Part C — The distinctions the firewall enforces

6. **Authority-preserving versus authority-creating display:** preserving display shows a source state in its own name or registered derivation with its accompaniments and its uncomfortable siblings visible; creating display adds approval, confidence, correctness, safety, completion, or urgency the source does not hold — by word, alias, glyph, colour, aggregate, prominence, affordance, or omission. The first is the only lawful kind.

7. **The register distinctions, carried in terms:** **review-only display** is human judgement shown as human judgement; **human-attributed display** names its reviewer-act source; **mechanical labels** state machine-decidable facts in closed vocabulary; **derived presentation labels** explain without softening or completing; **prohibited labels and prohibited visuals** exist only inside prohibitions. And the four confusions the firewall exists to make impossible: **absence is not unknown** (`unknown-not-absent` is a standing truth, never an empty cell); **pending is not converted** (a stub surfaced is a stub still pending); **evidenced is not anything from the `complian-` or `certif-` stems, nor legally satisfied** (an evidence pointer locates evidence and asserts no requirement satisfied); **executed is not passed** (one governed run happened; behavioural substance was not judged).

### Part D — The firewall rules, normative

8. **Twelve rules, binding on every future display artefact:**
   1. **A source state may be shown; display may not improve it** — no alias, summary, or visual may carry more than the state does.
   2. **A derived label may explain; it may not soften or complete** — explanation adds words, never certainty.
   3. **A non-authority notice travels with every execution, evidence, and applicability display**, in reach and unabbreviated.
   4. **Unknown may not be omitted or represented as absence** — it displays as a standing truth with its basis.
   5. **Deferred may not be represented as failure, progress, or completion** — it displays with its named dependency.
   6. **Pending may not be represented as action-ready conversion** — no affordance invites what only ceremony can do.
   7. **`behaviourally_executed` may not be represented as passed, tested, safe, correct, successful, or complete in behavioural substance** — it means one governed run happened, and its display says so.
   8. **`evidenced` may not be represented as legally satisfied, safe, production-ready, or by any word from the `complian-` or `certif-` stems** — the standing non-claim is part of the display.
   9. **Applicability-unresolved may not be represented as not-applicable** — unresolved means unresolved, at full size.
   10. **Future-owned may not be represented as absent, as a roadmap promise, or as soon** — dormant means dormant, with its gate named.
   11. **Visual semantics obey every rule above exactly as text does** — omission, prominence, grouping, sorting, iconography, colour, and interaction are claims, and each is checked as one.
   12. **A display artefact that cannot declare its labels, classes, derivations, visual semantics, coverage, and affordances as data cannot pass** — declarability is the firewall's admission condition, and an undeclarable artefact is unauditable and therefore unlawful.

### Part E — Proof: display never outruns source authority

9. **"Passing tests must not mint authority", translated to presentation:** a green suite, a silent scan, and a complete corpus run are facts about artefacts — they are never displayable as system approval, product endorsement, or a verdict about anything, and no aggregate view may convert them into one (V3, carried).

10. **Later artefacts prove firewall conformance deterministically, against their own declarations:** every display string belongs to the closed vocabulary with a valid class · every derivation used is registered and reviewed non-improving · the barred words and stems are absent from every display-string set · the declared glyph, colour-semantic, and affordance inventories contain no V1, V2, or V5 member · declared coverage includes every honest state family the artefact's sources hold, with no concealment default (V4) · every required accompaniment is present · no write path from presentation to source exists · and every ordering rule is declared and non-quality-named (V6, with the declared rule itself review-checked). **These obligations land with the artefacts that first bear strings and surfaces — the catalogue's validation under DR-W6-05's record and the surface proofs under DR-W6-06's contract — in the same-commit proof style the W5 runtime established.** Until such an artefact exists, there is nothing to check, and no conformance is claimed.

11. **The firewall promotes nothing.** No current source state is upgraded, reinterpreted, or repackaged by this record: the twenty-three fixtures remain `behaviourally_executed` and that still is not passed; the evaluation records' twenty-six unknowns remain unknown; Lane C's deferred rows remain deferred; the four applicability records remain unresolved; the nine stubs remain pending. A firewall that improved anything while forbidding improvement would be its own first breach.

### Part F — Boundaries

12. **This record does not decide:** detailed per-state rendering (DR-W6-02, inside this firewall) · review-routed delta presentation (DR-W6-03) · catalogue identity and the catalogue-ID namespace (DR-W6-05, whose validation consumes decisions 4, 5, 8 and 10) · the review-surface boundary contract (DR-W6-06, whose proofs consume decisions 4, 8 and 10) · any catalogue artefact · any final catalogue ID · any string grading · any rendered label · any UI or surface implementation.

13. **W6-D1 remains open; W6-D2 through W6-D6 remain unopened; W7 remains unopened.** Nothing here converts a stub, changes a status, touches a fixture, an evaluation record, Lane C, Tier 3 or applicability, contacts a model, or adds a dependency.

## Alternatives considered

- **A firewall as review guideline rather than mechanism (rejected).** Guidelines govern the careful; mechanisms govern everyone. Every bind in Part B is grounded in checkable structure.
- **Allowing bounded machine verdicts for "obviously mechanical" cases (rejected firmly).** The obviously-mechanical verdict is how authority minting starts; a machine output is a state or an observation, and where a verdict is ever lawful it is a recorded human act displayed as one.
- **Trusting derivations without a non-improvement review (rejected).** The derivation seam is the firewall's largest attack surface — it is exactly where a thesaurus becomes an authority press.
- **Exempting aggregates and dashboards as "just summaries" (rejected, ruled).** An aggregate is a derived label over many sources at once, and it obeys decision 5 like any other derivation — which is why an all-green anything cannot exist.
- **Deferring declarability to implementation taste (rejected).** Rule 12 is the admission condition: what cannot declare itself cannot be audited, and what cannot be audited does not ship.

## Consequences

- Every later W6 record now lands inside a stated guarantee: DR-W6-02 renders states the firewall already protects; DR-W6-05's catalogue and DR-W6-06's surfaces arrive knowing exactly which proofs they owe.
- The Wing's surfaces will be quiet by construction: states in their own names, doubts at full size, verdicts nowhere — because nowhere holds one.
- The hardest question of the surface era — "how do we know the screen isn't overclaiming?" — becomes a checklist over declarations rather than an argument over taste.
