**Five phases taught the Wing to hold the truth: laws, checks, a spine, walls, and a watcher that would not lie. Nothing yet speaks to a person. W6 is the phase where governed truth is finally shown — and the whole discipline of the era fits in one sentence, which is this phase's north star: _governed truth must survive display without becoming authority._ A rendered word is the same claim as a written one; a colour is a sentence; a checkmark is a verdict. ## 3. North star

**Governed truth must survive display without becoming authority.**

Unpacked, because every deliverable answers to it: a surface may show only what a governed source holds; the shown form may carry no more certainty, no more approval, and no more finality than the source carries; unknowns remain standing truths rather than gaps; deferrals remain named waits rather than failures or completions; execution state remains execution state; evidence state remains evidence state; and no arrangement of words, colours, icons, layout, grouping, or interaction may mint the authority that W0 Law 3 denies to confidence and ADR-0034 denies to green results. **Visual language is language**: everything the Wing's language law forbids a sentence to claim, it equally forbids a pixel to imply.

## 5. The doctrine questions W6-D1 must answer

Fourteen questions, which are one question asked fourteen ways — how does governed truth survive being displayed?

1. **What labels may the Wing display at all** — and is the display vocabulary closed, like every other Wing vocabulary?
2. **Which labels are stored and which are derived?** Lane C already rules its six-word assurance vocabulary *derived at presentation, never stored*; D1 generalises or bounds that pattern.
3. **Which labels are prohibited outright?** The barred lists exist in ink (ADR-0034 decision 39; ADR-0037 decision 11; the anti-overclaim families); D1 turns them into rendering law.
4. **How does `unknown` render without becoming absence** — a standing truth with its basis, never an empty cell, never an error to clear, never a gap that invites filling?
5. **How does review-required render without becoming machine judgement** — an invitation to a human, batched and never pushed, with no verdict attached?
6. **How does deferred render without becoming failure or completion** — the named dependency visible, the wait dignified?
7. **How does `behaviourally_executed` render without implying passed** — no green, no checkmark-as-verdict, no pass register anywhere near an execution status?
8. **How does `evidenced` render without implying conformance or certification** — the standing non-claim travelling with every pointer?
9. **How does `not_evidenced` render without shame and without false alarm** — the deliberate honesty rows staying dignified?
10. **How does applicability-unresolved render without implying not-applicable** — never a pass, never a dismissal?
11. **How are review-routed deltas shown to a human without a machine verdict** — both variant captures, the differing surfaces, and nothing graded on the instrument's behalf?
12. **How are pending stubs surfaced without conversion** — owner and unblocking condition visible, ceremony intact?
13. **How are carried open questions surfaced without retirement** — carried meaning carried, with owners, so the surface era cannot silently close them?
14. **How does label language — including visual language — remain unable to mint authority?** The candidate mechanism is already latent in the records: a closed display vocabulary, derived-not-stored presentation states, prohibited-wording law enforced by the woken catalogue-ID validator class, and language-law grading as an acceptance condition. The presentation-layer twin of "passing tests must not mint authority."

## 6. Risk bindings

Ten risks, each bound here so no W6 brief can meet it unprepared:

1. **Labels minting authority** — bound by the D1 label-authority firewall with structural proofs; the whole phase answers to §3.
2. **Visual overclaim** through colour, icon, layout, dashboard grouping, or checkmarks — bound by extending grading to design semantics: per-state rendering rules are doctrine, and the D5 assurance pass audits implied claims exactly as sentences are audited. **No green/pass/checkmark/verdict visual may attach to any execution, evidence, or applicability state.**
3. **Unknowns visually resolved, hidden, softened, or shown as errors** — bound by question 4's rendering law; omission of an honest state is itself a barred move; the twenty-six `unknown-not-absent` records are displayed only, never resolved, by anything in this phase.
4. **Stub conversion by convenience** — bound by §4's sequencing note: eligibility through W6 work, conversion only by separate ceremony per stub.
5. **Generative-era drift** — bound as a phase non-goal in §7: no model contact under any W6 deliverable; review surfaces render governed records only; the first-contact gate is ADR-0034's and belongs to no W6 deliverable.
6. **Catalogue scope creep** — bound by D1's catalogue identity law: catalogue IDs cover governed strings only; the thirty-three strings are the seed corpus; additions land through ceremony.
7. **Surface write-back** — bound by D1's review-surface boundary contract: read-only against governed sources, no state, no cache, no annotation store without its own future governed record; the presentation-layer twin of ADR-0024 decision 14.
8. **Historical register mismatch** — bound by routing the carried wording observations (the W1-era bounded ciphertext phrasing; the W3-era poetic register) to W6-D3 as language-register review items, **never pre-W6 or silent corrections to sealed records**.
9. **Dependency temptation** — bound by the standing rule: the zero-added-dependency record stands until a named fence-crossing record says otherwise, and any such crossing is its own explicit decision, never a milestone footnote.
10. **Board staleness at the phase boundary** — bound by this runway's own landing: the phase board's W6 section is refreshed in the same commit, per the standing same-commit currency discipline.
