**W7-D1 built a fence and named a crossing, and neither produced a sentence. W7-D2 designs the container a sentence would have to arrive in — while it is still empty, on purpose, because a shape argued over after the first interesting output exists is a shape argued over under pressure. This brief opens the workbench, not the vessel. Nothing it authorises holds any text, and nothing it authorises permits any.**

## 1. What this brief opens

**W7-D2 is open for record-shape work only, at this brief's acceptance, publication, and remote verification — and nothing else is.** *Opening is effective on publication:* while this brief's commit remains local it is not in force. W7-D2-B through W7-D2-E each require their own accepted authorisation; **W7-D3 through W7-D7 remain unopened**, and each opens only through its own brief.

## 2. The six standing statements

Binding at this brief's own landing, and repeated in every W7-D2 component that follows:

1. **W7-D2 creates a shape, not content.**
2. **W7-D2 authorises no model contact.**
3. **W7-D2 authorises no specimen.**
4. **W7-D2 authorises no generated-output import.**
5. **W7-D2 authorises no harness.**
6. **W7-D2 narrows none of W7-D3's four options.**

**A container is not an invitation.** A defined shape, a landed schema, a green validator and an empty directory authorise nothing.

## 3. The precondition this deliverable discharges, and the three it does not

A complete W7-D2 discharges **precondition 6** of ADR-0047 decision 9 — *the generated-output record shape exists and is accepted.* **It discharges nothing else.**

**Preconditions 2, 3 and 7 remain outstanding and are untouched by this deliverable:** the applicable Tier F crossing is W7-D3's; the named first-contact gate belongs to the brief of whichever deliverable would perform contact, and **no such gate is named anywhere**; the harness binding is W7-D4's. **After a complete W7-D2, three of the seven conjunctive preconditions still stand outstanding, and no model may be contacted.** Landing this brief moves none of them.

## 4. What W7-D2 inherits already decided, and may not reopen

**ADR-0046 whole** — synthetic-only by construction and the constructional test; capture is publication; the three lawful origins with a fourth unlawful; no-recirculation, capture terminal; the closed, growable-only exclusion list; no exemption for machine production; the boundary invariant and the non-naming law; provenance declarability as the admission condition; and the non-authority ceiling with specimen parity.

**ADR-0047 whole** — model contact, generated output and authored synthetic specimen defined by provenance rather than by vendor, medium or transport; a specimen is not model contact; provenance does not launder in either direction; **the governed handling binds on specimens and generated output identically**; the seven conjunctive preconditions; first contact one-way and non-retroactive, with a generated byte before its gate a stop-and-report event; and the fifteen-link anti-collapse chain.

**W7-D2 gives these a shape. It does not re-decide one word of them**, and no later component may narrow them by convenience.

**ADR-0047 decision 17** assigns W7-D2 **the finding-disposition mechanism**, within the finding-is-an-event principle, to be *"designed inside this principle rather than around it."*

## 7. Acceptance criteria for W7-D2

W7-D2 is complete when, and only when: **all five components are accepted and published through full ceremony, each before anything it gates**; the field shape assigned by ADR-0046 decision 21 and the mechanism assigned by ADR-0047 decision 17 are both discharged by the record that owns them; every obligation W7-D2-E specifies declares honestly whether a machine can decide it, with **nothing called mechanical that has not been demonstrated against real bytes** and any scope boundary disclosed in its record before landing; and **no generated text, no specimen, no record of the class, and no directory to hold one exists anywhere in the repository.** **A complete W7-D2 is a shape complete, not a class begun.**

## 8. Non-goals

W7-D2 does not: contact any model · produce, import, or hold generated text · author any specimen · create any record of the generated or specimen class · build, bind, or execute a harness · add any dependency · decide the model boundary, or narrow, rank, prefer or foreclose any of W7-D3's four options · create a run, a probe, or a fixture · fix the value set of any field reserved to a later deliverable · open W7-D3, W7-D4, W7-D5, W7-D6, or W7-D7 · open W8 · implement or anticipate private adoption · repair the carried W6 findings F-1 or F-2 · implement the optional audit proof module · convert any pending stub · resolve applicability · convert Tier 3 evidence · or move E10, Z4, E12/Z5, the hosted class, or the local class.

**P2 and Q3 remain review-only in full** — standing human-review duties at this landing and every later W7 landing, discharged by no test, no scan, and no suite. **ADR-0046's P3–P6 and ADR-0047's Q4 remain debts** until the first artefact of the generated or specimen class exists, and **this deliverable creates no such artefact.** No sentence in this brief or in its components claims safety, correctness, a pass, conformance, `certif-` status, `complian-` status, diagnosis, therapy, production readiness, approval, or behavioural proof — and **no medical, therapeutic, diagnostic, or crisis behaviour of any kind is built here or authorised by this brief.**

## 9. Public-safety considerations

No real health data, no clinical examples, no identified person, **no vendor or model named**, no URLs, no machine-identifying detail, no private lineage. Barred vocabulary appears only inside prohibitions, with the two scan-sensitive families carried as stems. **Nothing here is, or may be presented as, a safety, health, clinical, legal, or regulatory claim of any kind.**
