## 2. The laws W8-D4 carries, unweakened

4. **Only the human authority supplies a disposition** (ADR-0054 decision 18; ADR-0057 decision 6). The implementer may prepare, render, prove completeness, record returned tokens exactly and bind them, and may not recommend, infer, default, fill, bulk-select or substitute. The architect may clarify governing law only.
5. **The tokens are exactly** `governance_delta_present` · `no_governance_delta` · `review_inconclusive`, all three lawfully available on every case, with no default, recommendation, pressure or preselection.
6. **The human review question carries byte-identically from ADR-0054 decision 26:** *on the admissible source evidence alone, does a recognised governed property differ between the paired variants in a way that changes a downstream obligation, permission, authority, treatment or state?*
7. **Blinding, case independence and the conduct interval carry whole** (ADR-0054 decisions 27, 28 and 30; ADR-0057 decision 8 as reconciled by ADR-0059). Case ordering must not become hidden evidence, and no case-level judgment, preferred disposition or construction information reaches the reviewer from anyone else during a session's conduct interval.
8. **An authored intended disposition is a construction hypothesis, never an answer key** (ADR-0057 decision 21). **Counts only, each carrying its statement, and never a percentage, rate, score, grade, ranking, threshold, pass/fail result or readiness interpretation** (ADR-0054 decision 31; ADR-0057 decisions 22 and 23). A column of identical marks would itself be a finding, never a success.
9. **The published W8-D3 record is fixed.** Its published bytes and the human acts they record stay exactly as published: the register's dispositions were fixed at publication (ADR-0057 decision 12), and the revealed records are post-disposition calibration material only, never retroactive disposition evidence (ADR-0057 decision 21). **No W8-D4 act amends, re-dispositions or re-reveals any W8-D3 artefact.** A separately authorised diagnosis under section 7 may record new findings about a W8-D3 case's construction, doctrine or procedure, but **never rewrites that history**.
10. **ADR-0058 decision 5 is operative:** nothing from the void attempt becomes a source for any W8-D4 act. **The original corpus's retained records stay sealed** under their own authority (ADR-0059 decision 19), and no W8-D4 act lists, opens, parses, hashes, copies or reveals them.
11. **Committed bytes are the artefact** (`W8-D2-CBC`), and every validation custody act keeps the order of ADR-0057 Parts D and E as reconciled, carried by the route of section 5: reproduction from the exact retained bytes before any parse, a byte copy, a scan of the copies only, and only then a parse.
12. The nine anti-collapse rules carry whole:

> **confidence ≠ authority · verbosity ≠ governance significance · warmth ≠ permission · explanation ≠ evidence · textual difference ≠ governance difference · textual similarity ≠ governance equivalence · absence of explicit wording ≠ absence of governed effect · mechanical agreement ≠ human acceptance · uncertainty ≠ failure.**

## 3. What validation means here

13. **Validation deliberately challenges the instrument and records case-level comparison outcomes.** Its evidence is case-level: for each purpose-built validation case, the fixed human token beside the authored intended token, as `agreement` or `divergence`, exactly as ADR-0057 decision 20 defines the relation. **An agreement does not establish that the instrument detected anything, that either token is correct or that any case passed; a divergence is lawful signal about the case, the construction, the doctrine or the procedure — never a reviewer error** (ADR-0054 decision 28; ADR-0057 decisions 21 and 23).
14. **Validation never yields a verdict on the instrument.** It yields recorded case-level outcomes, organised by method, which the evidence-adequacy review of W8-D5 may later weigh under its own brief. Nothing in W8-D4 declares the instrument valid, mature, adequate or ready.

## 4. The six validation methods

15. **Each method is a construction discipline for fresh synthetic cases under ADR-0054 Parts A to D and ADR-0055, aimed at one way the instrument could fail.** Every validation case is still exactly one authored class — A, B or C — with its own withheld authoring record and commitment. The method is construction metadata, withheld until reveal like the class.

| Method | What it attempts | The failure mode it targets | Classes it may use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mutations** | a minimal edit to a fresh seed: one governed property changed (A), one surface feature changed with every property fixed (B), or the evidence a substantive answer needs removed (C) | insensitivity to a small governed change, or over-sensitivity to a small surface change | A, B, C |
| **Near-miss cases** | variants that come as close as the law allows to a governed-property threshold without crossing it (B), or just across it (A) | a threshold read by resemblance rather than by the downstream-obligation test (ADR-0054 decision 3) | A, B |
| **Deceptive stylistic variation** | surface features that suggest a governed change without making one — confident, warm or official wording (B) — and the counterpart, a quiet surface change that does alter a governed property (A) | confusing confidence with authority or warmth with permission, or treating surface classification as immunity (ADR-0054 decision 8) | A, B |
| **Under-specified cases** | evidence deliberately insufficient under each of ADR-0054 decision 11's four conditions, including cases that tempt an answer by elimination | defaulting to a substantive token where the law requires `review_inconclusive` (ADR-0054 decisions 11 and 12) | C |
| **Governance-preserving paraphrase** | heavy rewording, restructuring or reformatting with every governed property held fixed | treating textual difference as governance difference | B |
| **Boundary adversaries** | cases at the edges of the governed-property law: two properties interacting, an implicit change with no explicit wording, and differences in something that is not one of the nine recognised properties | missing an implicit governed effect, or resting a disposition on an unrecognised property | A, B, C |

16. **Boundary adversaries stay inside the nine recognised properties.** A difference in something the law does not recognise is authored only as a negative-control or inconclusive construction that tests whether the instrument wrongly treats it as governed; **no case may rest on, or extend, an unrecognised property** (ADR-0054 decision 2). If authoring reveals that the law needs a tenth property, that is a stop, recorded as an observation for separate authority.

## 5. Construction law to be fixed by the validation law

17. **Fresh seeds only.** Validation cases are authored freshly from public doctrine, never derived from, adapted from or templated on any published case of either corpus, and never from the void attempt. The published supplementary cases and their revealed construction records are known to the reviewer, so a validation case built from them would carry a recognition and influence risk that no comparison could separate from the instrument's behaviour. Textual non-reuse is proven in both directions against every published case, as the existing guards already require; **distinct text and identities support freshness, never proven semantic independence or absence of influence** (ADR-0059 decision 18; section 6).
18. **Method and family stay withheld.** A mutation family — a seed and its mutants — is a set of separate cases, each with its own identity, withheld record and commitment. Which cases share a family, and which method built each case, is construction information withheld until reveal. **The present withheld-record shape is closed (ADR-0055 decision 15), so the validation law must carry this metadata in a separately committed validation-construction record of its own, not by widening the existing record.** That record's own closed shape, bindings, commitment, custody and reveal rules are fixed by the validation law before any instance exists.
19. **Family members are recognisable to a reader even when their relation is withheld.** The validation law must decide how sessions treat that: either family members are separated across sessions, or they share a session and the record states that co-presence plainly, with case independence carried whole. Separation across sessions does not remove exposure already acquired at the corpus-candidate review; each session record states it (section 6).
20. **The corpus size is fixed by the validation law for workload and continuity only, never for statistical comparability**, and composition — class counts, method allocation and family structure — stays withheld until reveal.
21. **The governing laws are written for their own corpora.** ADR-0055's nonce, commitment, manifest and custody decisions (26 to 30) name the D2-C corpus and the supplementary corpus, and ADR-0057 as reconciled binds exactly the W8-D3 session corpus. **The validation law must therefore carry them to the validation corpus, whole and unweakened, by decisions of its own, leaving ADR-0055 and ADR-0057 byte-untouched.** The other route — an in-place reconciliation of those records, as ADR-0059 made — would amend them, which this brief does not authorise: it would need its own express authority and would move the existing proofs that pin their present text. The reviewer-visible `governed_by` field stays exactly as ADR-0055 decision 5 fixes it; the validation law states its own applicability through the corpus and session records and the registry's dependencies, never through a new reviewer-visible field, and maps each corpus-specific reference it carries.

## 6. Sessions, homes and evidence

22. **The human authority remains the sole reviewer.** Each validation session has its own conduct interval, as ADR-0057 decision 8 as reconciled defines it: from the reviewer's first access to any case material of that session's corpus, in any form and including review of the corpus's authoring candidate, until its session record is published, including the whole interval in which a returned token may still be revised. **The reviewer's prior exposure — to the revealed supplementary constructions, the W8-D3 comparison and this brief's method descriptions — is recorded in each session record and never attested away; no test proves its absence of influence.**
23. **W8-D4 uses homes and file names of its own**, distinct from every W8-D2, W8-S1 and W8-D3 home, so that every published corpus and W8-D3 artefact keeps its bytes and its historical guarantees. **Distinct homes do not remove the need for proof successions:** an existing proof moves only by an exact succession expressly authorised in its own landing (section 8), as R9's does at this one. W8-D4's validation corpus has its own custody sub-location inside the existing review transport, never named in any surface.
24. **The comparison and counts carry ADR-0057 Parts F and G, by the route of section 5.** Any organisation by method or family is a description of case-level outcomes, published only as counts each carrying its statement, and only if the validation law admits it. No rate, score or verdict is derived.

## 7. Diagnosis of divergence

25. **The W8-D3 completion record leaves its recorded divergence undiagnosed; its diagnosis belongs to a later governed act (ADR-0057 decision 21; `W8-D3-SCB` §7 item 31).** This brief proposes that diagnosis enters W8-D4, as its final landing, **after every W8-D4 validation session is published and its reveal is complete**, so that no diagnosis can shape a validation disposition. The same landing may diagnose any divergence the validation itself records. **If the human authority declines diagnosis, W8-D4 still completes at that landing**, with every divergence recorded and undiagnosed.
26. **Evidence limits.** A diagnosis rests only on published bytes: the case's two variants, its revealed withheld record, the published doctrine and law, and the published session records. **It never imports private conversation, unrecorded reasoning, model output or hidden context.** The human authority may add a verbatim, separately attributed observation of their own. No mechanical result establishes a diagnosis.
27. **The outcome set is closed.** Each diagnosed divergence is recorded with one or more of:
    - **construction** — the case's visible evidence does not lawfully support its authored class under the class authoring law;
    - **doctrine** — the doctrine admits more than one lawful reading of the case;
    - **procedure** — the packet, the review procedure or the session conditions bear on the outcome;
    - **undetermined** — the published evidence does not settle it.

    **No outcome attributes error to the reviewer (ADR-0054 decision 28), and no outcome declares either token correct.**
28. **Nothing is corrected inside W8-D4.** No disposition, case, manifest, withheld record, commitment or law changes as a result of a diagnosis. A construction, doctrine or procedure outcome that calls for change becomes a named finding for its own separately governed authority.
29. **Human decision points.** The human authority decides:
    - whether diagnosis enters W8-D4 at all;
    - who authors each diagnosis — the human authority alone, with the architect limited to a separately attributed mapping of the governing law and the implementer to mechanics;
    - the acceptance of each diagnosis record;
    - whether any finding is taken to separate authority.
