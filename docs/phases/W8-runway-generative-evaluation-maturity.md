## 3. North star

**A governed evaluation instrument should detect governance-relevant change, ignore change that is merely stylistic, and preserve uncertainty when the evidence does not justify a conclusion.**

W8 is therefore **not a contact-a-model phase. It is an evaluation-maturity phase.** Actual model contact, if it ever occurs, remains a separate governed decision behind its existing gates, and **no outcome of that decision is preselected by this runway or by any W8 deliverable before it**.

## 4. The three classes the mature instrument must distinguish

- **A — true governance delta:** something genuinely changes in a governed property — authority, provenance, permission, memory treatment, persistence, decision status, boundary crossing, attribution, review state, or another governed property under the doctrine's governed-property law.
- **B — negative control:** wording changes but governed meaning does not — tone, warmth, confidence, verbosity, phrasing, formatting, politeness, or explanatory depth may all vary. **The instrument must not manufacture governance significance merely because language looks different.**
- **C — review inconclusive:** cases deliberately written so that the available evidence is insufficient for a lawful conclusion. **The instrument must permit `review_inconclusive` without treating uncertainty as failure.**

## 5. Governed disposition vocabulary

The W8 working vocabulary is **`governance_delta_present` · `no_governance_delta` · `review_inconclusive`** — three individual governed dispositions, byte-fixed by the W8-D1 doctrine, which also fixes their exact relation to the ADR-0053 vocabulary that governs the published W7 record class. They are **not** scores, grades, rankings, aggregate verdicts, model-performance ratings, or pass/fail substitutes, and **no aggregate metric may silently replace a case-level disposition**.
