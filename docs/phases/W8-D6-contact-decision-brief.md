## 4. The decision question and the three outcomes

22. **The decision question is the runway's, in its own terms:** *on the published basis of section 3 alone, and with every standing gate of section 2 left exactly where it stands, does the human authority decide, for W8-D6, contact, deferral, or presently insufficient value?*
23. **The outcome form is closed.** The human authority's decision answers the runway's question with exactly one of:
    - **contact** — the human authority decides that actual model contact is to be pursued; this is a decision and not an authorisation, a schedule or a performance of contact: it authorises no contact, credential, provider, transport, prompt, harness, implementation or contact surface, selects no route or model class, and contact could occur only behind the separate instruments item 26 names, each needing its own authority, with all seven conjunctive preconditions of ADR-0047 Part D holding together;
    - **deferral** — the human authority decides that the contact decision is not taken now; no contact is pursued and nothing is prepared, the decision stays open for a separately governed act that reopens it under its own authority, and the deferral is complete as W8-D6's outcome, not a failure of W8 and not a decision against contact;
    - **presently insufficient value** — the human authority decides that actual model contact would presently add insufficient value; no contact is pursued and nothing is prepared, the decision is complete as W8-D6's outcome, it is not a finding about any model and not a failure of W8, and any later reconsideration would need its own governed act.

    **Each outcome is equally lawful, and none is preselected, expected or recommended by this brief. A deferral or a presently-insufficient-value outcome is complete, never a failure; a contact outcome authorises nothing.**
24. **Every decision states its reasons on the published basis, cites only basis rows by identity, path and commit, records the standing of every ADR-0047 Part D precondition unchanged, and records the limits that bound it.** A reason the basis cannot support is not given.
25. **No outcome is a finding about any model, a readiness, safety, correctness, approval, `certif-` or `complian-` claim, or a step towards contact;** no outcome moves ADR-0047 precondition 3; and no outcome is a Tier F crossing (section 5).
