## 6. Incident log

Four incidents occurred in W4. All are closed with final dispositions; **none remains open, and none created a closure blocker.**

1. **The Lane A landing stash incident.** During the Lane A landing a working-tree stash was taken outside the authorised landing steps; line-ending normalisation on restore corrupted the working tree, and a subsequent restore-from-index destroyed staged edits. Repaired byte-exact before any commit; **no governed byte was ever committed or published in a corrupted state.** *Standing practice carried forward: no stash, reset, checkout, or restore during a landing.*
2. **The transient real-contract test write.** A validator check under development wrote to the real Gym contract to construct a negative case, and newline translation altered the file. Detected and restored byte-exact; the check was redesigned to operate on in-memory text. **No governed byte was published in an altered state.** *Lesson: a validator never writes to the artefact it checks.*
3. **The test-runner environment drift and fence trip.** The test runner went absent from the machine between sessions and was reinstalled outside the repository. Its first run created a top-level cache directory, which **the repository's own directory-fence test rejected** — the fence doing precisely its job. The directory was removed and the suite is now run with caching disabled. **No governed state was affected**, and the trip was disclosed rather than smoothed. *Lesson: the repository's fences are load-bearing; an environment change is reported, not absorbed.*
4. **The shallow-copy guard misfire during erratum preparation.** A registry-entry preparation step mutated a shared nested structure through a shallow copy; **the preparation's own assertion caught it and aborted before any disk write**, and the step was redone with a deep copy. *Lesson: fail closed; a guard that stops the work before it touches disk is the guard working.*
