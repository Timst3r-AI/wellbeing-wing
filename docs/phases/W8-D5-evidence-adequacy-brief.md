## 4. What model contact could lawfully contribute, and what it could not

18. **Under ADR-0047 decision 3, text a model produces is generated output, governed by the published W7 record class, and the handling it receives is identical to an authored specimen's** (ADR-0047 decision 6). A W8 case exists only with a governed withheld authoring record that names its authored class and intended disposition (ADR-0054 decisions 6, 9 and 13; ADR-0055). **Generated material has no governed authoring record, no authored class and no intended disposition, so no construction hypothesis exists for it, and the agreement-or-divergence relation on which the whole W8-D3 and W8-D4 record rests cannot be formed for it.**
19. **Captured generated material cannot become a new W8 case or a calibration comparison.** Capture is terminal: a captured output may never become an input to another prompt, probe, fixture, catalogue string, doctrine record, summary or further contact (ADR-0046 decision 9; ADR-0047 decision 16). **What contact could lawfully contribute is therefore bounded: at most, real occasions for the governed handling, and human dispositions on them under the published W7 record class** — "The handling is the claim. The text is only the occasion" (ADR-0047 decision 7). **It could not contribute a harder calibration exam, a new W8 case, a comparison against any authored hypothesis, or evidence about any model;** the chain's link that model output is never evidence binds throughout.
20. **Lawful reception is addressed first.** Before any criterion is weighed, the finding record answers, in words, whether material a model produced could be received under the published doctrine, case shape and review procedure without changing them. **A negative answer is recorded as a named gap for separately governed authority under item 25, never repaired inside W8-D5, and the finding then answers the review question on the published evidence with that gap stated among its limits.** The threshold answer settles no finding form by itself.

## 5. The review question, criteria and finding form

21. **The review question, proposed for the review law to fix, is the runway's question in its bounded meaning:** *on the published evidence alone, is the instrument mature enough — adequate, in the meaning of item 4 — that actual model contact would add useful evidence: evidence of the kind section 4 shows contact could lawfully contribute, that the published synthetic record does not already supply, and that the instrument as published could lawfully receive?*
22. **The criteria are questions, not thresholds.** Each is addressed in words in the finding record; none is scored, weighted, ranked or reduced to a figure, and none determines the finding mechanically. They are exactly:
    - **discrimination** — what the published case-level comparisons record, read case by case beside the revealed construction records and described in words across the authored classes, under ADR-0060 decision 34: an agreement establishes no detection, and a divergence is no reviewer error;
    - **uncertainty** — whether `review_inconclusive` stayed structurally available in the packet, the procedure and the published record, and whether it occurs in the published record (ADR-0054 decisions 11 and 12); never whether any fixed disposition was correct;
    - **challenge** — what the published case-level records show, in words, of the six validation methods, and what they cannot show;
    - **limits** — the bounds the published record itself states and the limits of section 6, including that every recorded divergence stays undiagnosed;
    - **reception** — the threshold answer of item 20, carried into the finding: whether material a model produced could be received under the published doctrine, case shape and review procedure without changing them, given the identical governed handling of ADR-0047 decision 6;
    - **marginal value** — what actual contact could add that the published synthetic record cannot, what it could not add under section 4, and what the alternative routes of item 26 could add instead, read under the last link of ADR-0047 decision 18 in both directions;
    - **independence** — that the finding neither decides nor narrows the W8-D6 contact decision.
23. **The finding form is closed.** The human authority's finding answers the runway's question, in the bounded meaning of item 4, with exactly one of:
    - **mature enough** — on the published evidence, the instrument is mature enough that actual model contact would add useful evidence of the kind item 21 names; this is the bounded adequacy judgement, and no readiness, safety, correctness or approval claim;
    - **not mature enough** — on the published evidence, it is not: contact would add no useful evidence of that kind, because the published record already supplies what contact could lawfully contribute, or because what contact could contribute is not evidence about the instrument; this is not a failure of W8;
    - **undetermined** — the published evidence does not settle the question.

    **Each form is equally lawful, and none is preselected, expected or recommended by this brief. An undetermined finding is complete, never a failure.**
24. **Every finding states its reasons against each criterion, cites only admissible evidence by its published path and commit, and records the limits that bound it.** A criterion the evidence cannot answer is recorded as unanswerable, not as answered.
25. **A finding may name a gap — a needed doctrine, case-shape or procedure change, including a reception gap under item 20 — only as a named finding for separately governed authority. Nothing is corrected inside W8-D5.** The forms describe evidence only: none is a contact decision, and the W8-D6 options remain W8-D6's own.
26. **Alternative evidence routes are named so that the finding is not read against an empty comparison.** Besides contact, the limits of section 6 could be addressed by a second human reviewer, which ADR-0054 decision 18 does not provide for; by a diagnosis of the recorded divergences; by a corpus authored by a different author; by synthetic cases shaped to the Wing's own domain; or by a further session of the same reviewer on the existing corpora. **Each is a possibility requiring its own separate authority; none is authorised, scheduled, recommended or foreclosed by this brief, and the marginal-value criterion weighs contact beside them, not alone.**

## 6. Limitations this review carries

27. **The review cannot exceed its evidence.** It establishes nothing about any model's behaviour, quality, safety or correctness; nothing about the reviewer's independence or the absence of influence; no statistical comparability; no semantic independence of any case; no answer key; and no diagnosis of any divergence.
28. **The evidence is one human reviewer's acts on purpose-built synthetic cases,** in corpora sized for workload rather than statistics, with recorded prior exposure and family co-presence, all constructed by the same governed process, **and every recorded divergence stays undiagnosed, so the review weighs divergences as recorded, never as explained.** These are limits to state, never defects to repair inside W8-D5.
29. **An undiagnosed divergence cuts in no direction.** Each recorded divergence is equally consistent with a cause in the construction, the doctrine, the procedure or none determinable, so the discrimination criterion can describe the record but infer nothing about the instrument from any divergence, in either direction; and an agreement establishes no detection (ADR-0060 decision 34, carrying ADR-0057 decision 21).
30. **The sole reviewer assesses their own record.** The human authority who returned every disposition, accepted every record and adopts any assisted reading is the authority who makes this finding. That is the design (ADR-0054 decision 18), and it is a limit stated here, not repaired: any separately attributed assistance is published with the finding record before its acceptance, so that the basis of the finding can be read beside it.
31. **Mechanical checks establish structural facts only** (ADR-0054 decision 22): byte identity and hash equality prove that the evidence is what was published, never what it means.
32. **AI assistance is attributed, never substituted.** Any assisted reading the human authority considers is recorded as assistance, with its provenance limits; adopting it is the human authority's express act, recorded as such.
