## 9. The neutral A/B/C/D assessment frame

### 9.5 Option D discipline — both sides, stated

**Option D is not model contact and must never be described as it.** A specimen is *text written by a human inside this repository, from nothing, deliberately carrying the properties of generated prose* — ADR-0049 decision 10, ADR-0047 decision 3(c). **[law]**

**What D can prove.** Governed handling of authored specimens **under the same handling law as generated output** — ADR-0047 decision 6 binds the handling identically, so these are not weaker governance tests merely because the prose was authored; the provenance differs, the handling does not. **[law]** Concretely: pair completeness · finding creation · non-landability · Part Q behaviour · stop-and-report · manifest integrity · proof succession · lifecycle mechanics · review routing · and **the deliberate construction of difficult failure cases rather than waiting for one to occur.** **[inference]**

## 10. Scan-environment reproducibility law

**The fact to be governed.** The public-safety scanner loads an optional local wordlist **once**, in `main()`, and applies the same list to every scanned file in either mode; a `local-wordlist` finding is appended directly and is **never allowlistable**. The branch is active only when the **effective** list — after stripping blank and comment lines — is non-empty, and the scanner's own summary reports `present` / `absent (normal)` from that effective list. **A backing file may exist and the branch still be inactive.** **[mechanism]**

**W7-D3 owns the requirement; W7-D4 owns its representation in the run manifest.** **[inference on routing; the reproducibility posture is D3's by `W7-AR` §8]**

**The governed run fact is exactly one bit:**

> **capture-time local-wordlist branch state: `active` or `inactive`**, determined from the effective filtered list the scanner actually used.

**Nothing more may be recorded.** Not whether a backing file exists · not its path beyond already-public scanner doctrine · not a term count · not a term · not a fingerprint or hash of a term · not an excerpt · not machine identity · not any other private configuration. **[law: `W7-AR` §7's prohibition; ADR-0046's boundary invariant]**

**W7-D3 must classify this one bit explicitly as public run provenance rather than private machine configuration**, and say so in terms that survive later quotation. **[inference]**

**The dormancy rule, which is the point of recording it at all:**

> **A run whose branch was `inactive` establishes that the local-wordlist path was dormant for that run. It does not discharge the coordinate seam, and repeated inactivity never accumulates into resolution.**

**W7-D7 may not treat repeated inactivity, or an absence of findings, as resolution.** **[inference, applying the W7-D2-E governing rule verbatim: *absence may prove dormancy; absence may not discharge a debt whose subject does not exist*]**

### 11.1 Manifest artefact shape — routed to W7-D4

ADR-0048 requires **one registered manifest per run with a content hash for each record**, and fixes what it is: an integrity index and run inventory — *not a summary, not a conclusion, not a scoreboard*, and no field carrying a verdict about a run (decision 21). **[law]** ADR-0048 designs no manifest; ADR-0049 also disclaims designing one, and **neither assigns the shape to a named later deliverable.** **[law, by absence]**

**Consequence:** W7-D2-E could implement S2 only as the **set-and-hash relation over neutral identity tokens**, encoding no manifest key, entry object, field name or parser — because there was no lawful shape to bind to. **[law: the W7-D2-E record's §4 boundary]** **D5 cannot produce a landable record without a manifest.** **[inference]**

### 11.2 Part Q — routed to a W7-D4 pre-execution ceremony

ADR-0050 decisions 53–55: under the landed law, **any capture producing a scan finding cannot be published as a GER** — *"including patterns a model is comparatively likely to produce, and which the evaluation exists in order to observe"* — and the two candidate resolutions are *"decisions for the human authority and the architect, not for a mechanism record"*, each needing its own ceremony. **[law]**

**W7-D4 must either resolve the seam through its proper authority, or explicitly accept the narrowing and prove that D5 execution remains lawful under it. It must not be discovered during execution.** **[inference on scheduling; the ownership is ADR-0050's]**

**The no-reliance rule, which is what keeps Part Q's later ownership from distorting the D3 decision.** **W7-D3 must assess and select among A, B, C and D against the Part Q law as it stands at the moment of the D3 decision.** Today that law is exactly this: **no finding-bearing GER is landable.** **[law: ADR-0050 decisions 53–54]**

> **The W7-D3 decision may not rely on, assume, price in, discount for, or pre-authorise any future relaxation or resolution of Part Q.**

**Part Q stays routed to W7-D4 and W7-D4 does not move ahead of W7-D3.** W7-D4 may later resolve the seam through its proper ceremony, or explicitly accept the narrowing — but **a future doctrine may not retroactively subsidise an option chosen today.** An option that is only attractive on the assumption that Part Q will later be relaxed is, at the moment of choosing, an option chosen on a fact that does not exist. **[inference]**

### 11.3 `local-wordlist` locus production — routed to W7-D4

ADR-0050 requires every finding to carry a lawful 1-based `character_start` counted in Unicode code points of the decoded capture, and records that the current `local-wordlist` semantics **do not guarantee** such a coordinate — for two independent reasons, occurrence ambiguity and case-folding expansion. It names three possible owners for a fix and **chooses between none of them.** **[law]**

**W7-D3 must not choose the mechanism.** W7-D4 may assess a scanner change, a governed capture-time resolver, another mechanism, or a pre-capture stop posture. **[inference on routing]**

**Standing rule, binding on any later execution:**

> If the effective branch is **naturally inactive**, the seam is **dormant for that run only** — dormant is not resolved.
> If the effective branch is **active**, it may not be disabled, ignored, emptied, renamed, bypassed or otherwise neutralised to make execution succeed.
> If it produces a finding before lawful locus production exists: **STOP AND REPORT.**

**Manufacturing absence is barred.** Capture-time scanning is prior precisely because **capture is publication**, and a later tracked-file rejection cannot retroactively repair a capture-time governance failure. **[law: ADR-0046 capture-is-publication; ADR-0050's order of operations]** **[inference: the no-manufactured-absence rule is this brief's formulation of that law applied to the optional branch]**

### 11.5 W7-D2-E proof transition — W7-D4 designs, W7-D5 performs

**Three published W7-D2-E test functions become inapplicable when the authorised creation interval ends:** `test_s1_reserved_home_is_vacant`, `test_no_generated_evaluation_record_exists`, `test_absence_does_not_discharge_record_dependent_debts`. **W7-D2-E is not thereby erroneous and requires no erratum.** Its statements were true when it landed: there were zero GERs and the debts genuinely were outstanding. **State evolution is not error**, and an erratum belongs only where a record was wrong at its landing. **[precedent: ADR-0003's Tier M erratum path, which exists for records that were incorrect, and the live W6-D4-SMR erratum, which corrected a fact that was wrong on the day]**

## 15. First-contact gate posture

**Precondition 3 is preserved exactly and is not discharged here.** It requires a named first-contact gate **in the brief of the deliverable that would perform contact** — ADR-0047 states this is *"not this record, and not the W7-D1 brief"*, and the same reasoning excludes this one. **[law]**

**W7-D3 performs no contact under any option it may select.**

**The performing deliverable is not automatically W7-D5**, and this brief does not assume it. The dependency runs the other way: **whichever deliverable would perform contact must have its own accepted brief containing the named gate before contact occurs.** Under the runway's sequence that is most naturally W7-D5, because D5 is the execution deliverable — but *most naturally* is not *named*, and **a gate that has not been written into an accepted brief does not exist.** **If W7-D3 selects Option D, no deliverable performs contact, and precondition 3 is never exercised.** It stands **outstanding** — not waived, not deemed inapplicable, not satisfied by analogy, and not failed. **The seven preconditions are prerequisites to contact; they are not a requirement that public W7 contact a model before it may close.** **[law: ADR-0047 decisions 9–11]**

**That is a lawful no-contact resting state, and the anti-collapse chain says so in its own words.** Its fifteenth and final link is **`no contact ≠ nothing proven`**, and ADR-0047 decision 20 makes the link load-bearing in both directions: *“Choosing not to contact a model proves nothing about a model — and it does not mean W7 proved nothing. **W7-D7 must still report precondition 3 honestly as outstanding.** A lawful resting state is not a discharged one, and a closure record that blurred the two would fail in the opposite direction — by implying a gate had been passed that was never approached. **[inference]**
