## 4. Closure without false resolution

This section is binding on the D7 assessment and closure record. It consumes, rather than invents, the standing W2-through-W6 closure standard restated in `W6-D6-CAB` §1.3: a phase may close complete with open carried items **if and only if each is named, classified, and never represented as resolved**. 5. **Phase completion and universal resolution are not the same proposition.** W7 may close while a matter remains open where the governing corpus already permits that matter to remain open and the phase has not falsely represented it as discharged.

6. **An outstanding conditional gate is not a defect merely because its triggering act did not occur.** ADR-0047 precondition 3 must remain **outstanding in its lawful resting state** unless a contact-performing deliverable actually exercises the gate. D7 may not waive it, discharge it, mark it inapplicable, or call it failed.

7. **An unresolved but bounded seam remains unresolved.** Part Q and the `local-wordlist` coordinate seam must be carried exactly as the current governing records leave them. D7 may describe their bounds and current posture; it may not solve them by closure language.

8. **Historical unknowns remain questions unless their own governing evidence resolves them.** The twenty-six generative-era unknowns used as W7 exam material remain historical unknowns. Neither authored specimen construction nor twenty-six `governance_delta_present` human-review dispositions answer those unknowns about any model.

9. **A human-review disposition is not a model verdict.** D7 must preserve the ADR-0053 meaning of every disposition. Twenty-six identical tokens are twenty-six individual human acts, not a score, pass rate, model grade, variant preference, or aggregate conclusion.

10. **Seam-register law survives phase boundaries, but seam states must be reported precisely.** Landing B must re-derive all six seams from the governing source, not from the phase board or session memory. The four HARD seams are timing prerequisites that must already have been discharged at or before their named moments for D5 materialisation to have been lawful; at closure they are accounted as **discharged timing gates with named published evidence**, never as unfinished carried matters. The two CARRYABLE seams remain carried on their source-defined terms unless a later governed record has changed them. If Landing B cannot prove any HARD seam was lawfully discharged, that is a stop-and-report closure defect, not an accepted limitation.

11. No item may move from `outstanding`, `unresolved`, `carried`, `external`, `deferred`, or equivalent source state to a closed state merely because the phase is closing.
