## 1. What this brief opens, and what it does not

1. On acceptance, publication and independent remote verification, this record opens **W8-D7 only**, for the programme of section 4. **No later phase is opened, designed, scheduled or named as assigned**; the gate pattern of `W7-CR` §10 — three documents, three acceptances — would govern any next phase, and W8-D7 supplies none of them.
2. **Effective W8-D7 does not mean** that W8 is finished, that any closure sentence is accepted, that any inventory is settled, or that any inherited, unresolved or carried matter has moved. **Closure of W8 exists only after the W8 closure record of section 4 has itself been accepted, published and independently remote-verified.**
3. **The deliverable is the runway's, carried exactly:** "Whole-phase source re-derivation and closure under the discipline established through W2–W7" (runway §6), landing only after the evidence is published and independently reviewable (runway §6.1). **Every W8 deliverable before it has completed and been independently verified at the baseline**, and closure preparation is therefore lawful.
4. **Completion of the governed programme and a conclusion about the instrument are different propositions.** Closure may state that W8 completed the governance work its accepted runway and briefs assigned to it. It may not state, imply or summarise that the instrument has reached maturity, adequacy, validity, readiness, safety or usefulness, that any model would behave in any way, that any adoption is fit or near, or that contact has become warranted or foreclosed. None of those propositions was established by any W8 act, and the one W8 act that asked a question of that kind — the evidence-adequacy review — answered that the published evidence does not settle it.

## 2. The closure question, and the claim closure may test

5. The question of W8-D7 is not whether W8 can be made to look finished. It is:

> **Can W8 close because its own governed programme is complete and publicly evidenced, while the W8-D5 finding stays undetermined as recorded, the W8-D6 decision stays a decision that authorises nothing, every separate contact instrument stays unopened, every standing contact precondition stays unchanged, every recorded divergence stays undiagnosed, and every inherited and unresolved matter stays named at its true standing?**

6. **The final W8 closure record may claim no more than the following, and only if the published assessment supports every limb:**

> **W8 completed the governance work assigned to it under its accepted runway and deliverable briefs, in the runway's order — a doctrine before any case, an instrument before calibration, calibration before validation, validation before the adequacy review, the review before the contact decision — each landed by its own accepted brief and authorisation and each human act recorded under law that preceded it; the public/private boundary held; no W8 act prepared, authorised or performed model contact, and the published W8 records, under ADR-0058's standing reservation, classify no exchange under ADR-0047 decision 3 and do not state whether model contact occurred; and every finding, decision, divergence, precondition, instrument, seam, carried item and historical unknown that remains undetermined, unopened, undiagnosed, outstanding or unresolved is named and represented as resolved nowhere.**

7. **The claim is about governed handling**, not about any model, not about the instrument's maturity and not about any person. The closure record may not claim or imply that the instrument discriminates, that any disposition was correct, that any model passed, failed, behaved safely or correctly, complied with anything, is production-ready, has clinical validity or legal conformance, is approved, or suitable for any person or use, or that any adoption is warranted.
8. **The W7 closure's contact sentence is not available to W8 in W7's form.** `W7-CR` could state that no model was contacted at any point; every W8 record since ADR-0058 carries instead its Part F reservation, which classifies no exchange under ADR-0047 decision 3 and does not state whether model contact occurred. Closure carries that reservation exactly, deciding it in neither direction, and states what the record does support: **no W8 act prepared, authorised or performed model contact, and no W8 record classifies any exchange.**
9. The anti-collapse chain of ADR-0047 decision 18 carries whole, and its last link holds in both directions here as it did at the W7 closure (ADR-0047 decision 20):

> **named ≠ authorised · authorised ≠ scheduled · scheduled ≠ performed · specimen ≠ contact · contact ≠ capability · generated ≠ said · fluent ≠ correct · plausible ≠ true · two variants ≠ a better variant · difference ≠ defect · captured ≠ endorsed · routed ≠ reviewed · reviewed ≠ approved · model output ≠ evidence · no contact ≠ nothing proven.**

## 3. Closure without false resolution

10. **This section is binding on the closure assessment and the closure record.** It consumes, rather than invents, the standing W2-through-W7 closure standard restated in `W6-D6-CAB` §1 and applied by `W7-D7-CAB` §4: a phase may close complete with open carried items **if and only if each is named, classified, and never represented as resolved.** W8 applies that standard to a phase whose central open matters are a finding that is undetermined by the human authority's own answer, a decision that by its own terms authorises nothing, and instruments that are named and not supplied.
11. **Phase completion and universal resolution are not the same proposition.** W8 may close while a matter remains open where the governing corpus already permits that matter to remain open and the phase has not falsely represented it as discharged.
12. **The W8-D5 finding is undetermined, complete and fixed.** It is the human authority's bounded answer to one fixed question on one fixed index (`W8-D5-AFR` Parts D and H; ADR-0061 decisions 16 and 18), and W8-D7 carries it exactly as recorded: not as a failure, not as adequacy or inadequacy, not as a readiness or maturity statement in either direction. No W8-D7 act reopens, re-weighs, re-reads, supplements or summarises the finding into a conclusion the record does not carry, and no W8-D7 act makes a second adequacy finding.
13. **The W8-D6 decision is contact, as the human authority's stated choice, and it authorises nothing.** W8-D7 carries the decision at its published standing (`W8-D6-CDR` Parts B, D and E): a decision and not an authorisation, a schedule or a performance of contact; its reasons and its disposition of the separate instruments the human authority's own by express adoption of separately attributed assistance, carried with their attribution; the four separate instruments — a separately accepted implementation brief naming the first-contact gate, a Tier F crossing record of its own, every ADR-0047 precondition holding together, and the governed handling of anything captured — **named, not supplied and left unopened**; and the question which governed act could hold an implementation brief **named and unanswered**, with no performing deliverable existing in the published law. No W8-D7 act opens, schedules, prepares, ranks or pre-positions any of them, treats the decision as a step taken, resolves whether or where their authority is sought, or supplies the absent performing deliverable by an interpretation of sequencing latitude. The reported authority gap stays reported.
14. **An outstanding conditional gate is not a defect merely because its triggering act did not occur.** ADR-0047 precondition 3 remains **outstanding in its lawful resting state** unless a contact-performing deliverable actually exercises the gate; W8-D7 may not waive it, discharge it, mark it inapplicable or call it failed, and no other precondition moves (ADR-0047 decision 11).
15. **A recorded divergence stays recorded and undiagnosed.** By the human authority's decisions at the W8-D3 and W8-D4 completions (`W8-D3-CCR`; `W8-D4-CPR`), every divergence stays exactly as its comparison record published it; W8-D7 diagnoses, explains away or corrects none, assigns no outcome of `W8-D4-IVB` item 27 to any case, declares no token correct, names and characterises no case, and keeps ADR-0058's R9 board sentence as published. **This is the standing non-diagnosis condition, and it survives closure.**
16. **The void attempt stays void and the reservation stays.** ADR-0058 decision 5 is operative: nothing exposed or returned in the void attempt is a source for any governed state, and no W8-D7 record cites, binds or characterises it. W8-D7 classifies no exchange under ADR-0047 decision 3 and does not state whether model contact occurred; Q3 remains a fresh human-review duty at every landing.
17. **The retained records stay sealed.** The original corpus's retained records stay under ADR-0059 decision 19, and every corpus's custody stays under its own existing authority; no W8-D7 act accesses custody or lists, opens, parses, hashes, copies, reveals or names any retained record or custody location.
18. **Historical facts are preserved at full size.** The committed-blob proof correction, the manifest-shape amendment, the historical-pin and historical-absence proof corrections, the case-home scope correction, the documentation erratum, the identity remediation and the reconciliations ADR-0059 records stay in the public history as they happened; no earlier record is rewritten, no erratum leaves its entry, and no correction is tidied into a smoother account.
