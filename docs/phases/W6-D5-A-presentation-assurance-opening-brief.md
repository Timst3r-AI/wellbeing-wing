## 1. Opening statement

1. **W6-D5 is open for brief-governed assurance work only.** The programme question is exactly: **did the showing stay honest?** D5 does not close the phase (W6-D6's), does not redesign or repair the surface, and opens nothing. **W6-D6 and W7 remain unopened.**

2. **The audited baseline is clean and named:** the static review surface at `docs/surface/review-surface.html` with its declarations and its 188-row trace, twenty-six structural proofs green, and the published Tier M erratum reconciling the materialisation record's proof-count wording to the live suite. **This audit reads that baseline; it does not alter one byte of it.**

## 2. The D5 sequence

3. **A** — this record. **B+C+D+E** — the audit execution as one landing: rendered-state audit, implied-claim audit, catalogue-to-surface and source-to-surface trace audit, and the W6-owned pending-stub reassessment, merged because they are one read-only session over one artefact set sharing a single evidence base, and splitting them would republish the same evidence four times through three partially-reported intermediate states. **F** — findings consolidation with correction paths named, separate. **G** — closure posture, separate, after the findings are published and independently reviewable. **F and G are kept apart deliberately**: where an audit records anything correction-needed, the findings must be visible to Tara and Ari before completeness is declared over them.

## 3. The standing audit directive, carried

4. **State-family audit reasoning is used only where an assurance rule is genuinely shared**, with each shared rule tied to its source authority (ADR-0040's per-state rendering laws; ADR-0041's routing, stub and question laws; ADR-0043's contract and declarability rule). **Every rendered item, trace row, and stub finding is audited individually and published as its own row.** **Family reasoning explains; the per-item audit row answers.** No family verdict may stand in for an item's verdict, and no shared rule may excuse an individual mismatch, omission, overclaim, visual implication, trace drift, or source contradiction from being found and named.

## 4. Risks bound at opening

5. **Rendered-state drift** — a displayed state having wandered from what its source holds → every rendered state is compared, per item, to the governed source it claims. **Visual semantics minting authority** — colour, glyph, order, prominence, grouping, or the very silence of a page without controls implying approval, completion, or review → each is audited as language, including the silence. **Trace mismatch** — a row rendered untraced, a row traced unrendered, or a trace field emptied → the bijection and the mandatory fields are checked in both directions, per row. **Omission overclaim** — an uncomfortable state quietly absent while its siblings render → coverage is audited against the sources, not against the page's own table of contents. **Catalogue/display confusion** — a catalogue entry read as permission to display, or a grade read as approval → the ceilings are audited where they must appear. **Pending-stub conversion by implication** — a stub's posture drifting toward "done" because later work exists → the reassessment distinguishes *eligible for future consideration* from *converted*, and **converts nothing**.

## 5. What this deliverable's assurance is not

6. **D5 assurance is not certification, not safety, not production readiness, not clinical validity, not legal conformance, not correctness, and not approval of anything.** A clean audit says one thing: under the W6 rules, the presentation remained faithful to its sources on the checks performed, on the day performed. It says nothing about the world, about any person, or about fitness for any use. **No finding, and no absence of findings, may ever be presented otherwise.**

## 6. Boundaries

7. **This record does not:** audit anything · record any finding · classify anything · reassess any stub · declare D5 complete · correct, redesign, or touch the surface, the generator, the proof module, the catalogue, the grading records, the trace, any source record, or the pending ledger · open W6-D6 or W7. **Any correction the audit finds necessary is named, not made** — its smallest governed path is proposed, and execution waits for its own authorisation.
