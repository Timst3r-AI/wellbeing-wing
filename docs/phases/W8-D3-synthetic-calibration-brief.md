## 2. The laws D3 carries, unweakened

4. **Only the human authority supplies a disposition** (ADR-0054 decision 18). The human authority, acting as the human reviewer, supplies each of the twelve case-level dispositions individually. The implementer may prepare, prove completeness, render whole and symmetric, record returned tokens exactly and bind them mechanically — and may not recommend, infer, default, fill, bulk-select or substitute. The architect may give source-grounded clarification of governing law only — never of case content, and never a recommendation, ranking or preferred outcome.
5. **The available tokens are exactly** `governance_delta_present` · `no_governance_delta` · `review_inconclusive`, all three lawfully available on every case, with no default, recommendation, pressure or preselection (ADR-0054 decisions 14 and 26).
6. **The human review question carries byte-identically from ADR-0054 decision 26:** *on the admissible source evidence alone, does a recognised governed property differ between the paired variants in a way that changes a downstream obligation, permission, authority, treatment or state?*
7. **Each case is reviewed independently, from its reviewer-visible evidence, read whole and symmetrically** (ADR-0054 decisions 20, 27 and 30). The admissible source evidence is the two variants and nothing else.
8. **Blinding holds from the reviewer's first access to any case material of the session corpus, in any form, until the session record is published.** No authored class, intended disposition, construction map, authoring check, rationale, inconclusive-condition selection, nonce, expected distribution, class count, aggregate, recommendation, default, ranking, highlight, summary, diff or mechanical inference — and no case-level judgment, preferred or predicted disposition or rationale about any case from anyone other than the reviewer — may reach the reviewer in that interval, including while a returned token may still be revised (ADR-0054 decisions 27–28; ADR-0055 Part K; ADR-0057 decision 8 as reconciled by ADR-0059).
9. **An authored intended disposition is a construction hypothesis, never an answer key and never a certified correct answer** (ADR-0055 decision 16; `W8-D2-DIB` §7.18).
10. **Mechanical evidence never substitutes for human semantic judgement.** No proof decides, predicts or hints at any disposition; no green result is a review act.
11. **Withheld authoring material stays outside every reviewer-visible surface until the lawful reveal point** of section 3.4.
12. **Before any reveal and before any calibration comparison, every published authoring commitment of the session corpus — and of no other corpus — is reproduced by hashing the exact retained bytes directly, before any parse or reserialisation.** One missing record, one altered byte or one mismatch is **STOP AND REPORT**, with no reconciliation path (ADR-0055 decision 29).
13. **Revealed authoring material is post-disposition calibration material only.** It never retroactively becomes evidence for, replaces or amends a disposition already made (ADR-0054 decisions 19 and 23; `W8-D2-DIB` §6.16).
14. **Disagreement between authored intent and a human disposition is lawful signal about the case or the instrument** — never automatically a reviewer error, never a silent correction opportunity in either direction; any resolution is its own governed act (`W8-D2-DIB` §7.18; ADR-0054 decision 28).
15. **Counts only, and only after all twelve individual dispositions exist** (ADR-0054 decision 31): never a percentage, rate, score, grade, ranking, threshold, pass/fail result, model-performance claim or readiness interpretation.
16. **Committed bytes are the artefact.** Every D3 rendering of, and every D3 proof over, the immutable published D2 cases and commitment manifest — and, under the same discipline, the immutable published cases and manifest of the session corpus — reads their committed Git object bytes, as the effective `W8-D2-CBC` requires; a checkout's line-ending rewrite is display (ADR-0055 decision 10). D3 consumes that rule; it does not own or repeat the correction.
17. The nine anti-collapse rules carry whole wherever they are carried — abbreviation is collapse by other means:

> **confidence ≠ authority · verbosity ≠ governance significance · warmth ≠ permission · explanation ≠ evidence · textual difference ≠ governance difference · textual similarity ≠ governance equivalence · absence of explicit wording ≠ absence of governed effect · mechanical agreement ≠ human acceptance · uncertainty ≠ failure.**

## 3. The W8-D3 programme — four landings, each behind its own acceptance

18. **Why four, in this order — inherited law and this brief's own safeguard, kept distinct.** *Inherited law* requires that reveal and calibration proceed only under the later calibration law ADR-0054 decision 28 anticipates; that every required disposition is recorded before any reveal; and that every commitment of the corpus being revealed is reproduced from the exact retained bytes before any reveal or calibration comparison (ADR-0055 decision 29). *Precedent*, not strict necessity, places that law in its own landing before the session, as ADR-0053 preceded the W7 dispositions and ADR-0055 preceded the first case. **And this brief adds one deliberate W8-D3 safeguard that inherited law does not itself require: D3-C must be published and independently remote-verified before D3-D touches any withheld byte beyond the existence-only check**, so that the dispositions are immutable in public before anything they could be compared with is read. The comparison is mechanical from published bytes and adds no human act, so reveal, comparison and completion travel together in D3-D.

## 4. Review-packet law (for ADR-0057 to fix)

19. **No adequate machinery exists today.** The W6 surface generator is bound to the string catalogue, and the W7-D6 review packet was generated outside the repository by a renderer that was never tracked. D3 therefore needs a **narrowly governed session packet**, not a new public review surface.
20. The packet is rendered **deterministically by a published renderer from the committed Git object bytes** of the twelve published cases of the session corpus, at its pinned commit, under `W8-D2-CBC` — never from working-tree copies — and its SHA-256 is recorded in `W8-D3-HDR`, so anyone can regenerate and verify it.
21. It presents: each case in **ascending lexical `case_id` order**, one case per section with no cross-reference; the case identity; **both variants whole, verbatim and whitespace-preserving** (some variants contain line breaks, so no markup interpretation may restructure them), labelled only `variant_a` and `variant_b`, with **identical formatting for both**; the review question byte-identical; the three tokens in one fixed order, identical on every case, with **no default and no pre-marked choice**; and a response line for every case.
22. **It omits `structural_provenance_support`.** That object remains present in the governed case artefacts, but it is support material, not semantic grounding, and is not shown in the packet. The packet also omits any diff, highlight, summary, length or similarity figure, manifest digest, ordering rationale, running tally, token count or progress-by-token, and anything derived from withheld material.
23. The session is the reviewer's: reading order within the packet is free; revision before returning is free; **the set is returned complete — twelve tokens, one per case — or the session is incomplete.** An incomplete session records nothing and reveals nothing; D3 waits.

## 5. The disposition register and record (for ADR-0057 to fix)

24. **Register:** one closed canonical-bytes JSON object, schema byte-fixed (proposed `w8-disposition-register-v1`), rows sorted solely by `case_id`, each row exactly the case identity and the one returned token. **It carries no authored class, intended disposition, construction map, authoring check, rationale, inconclusive-condition selection, confidence, per-row timestamp or other withheld authoring metadata.** Once published, the human tokens can be counted mechanically — that distribution is a distribution of human acts, published as counts only under section 7, and it discloses nothing withheld.
25. **Record:** the act's date, the reviewer role, the packet SHA-256, the register binding, the session-corpus binding (authoring-landing commit, home tree identity, manifest SHA-256), the reviewer's procedural attestation under ADR-0059, the statement that every token is an individual human act, and — only if the reviewer volunteers one — a verbatim, non-disposition, non-evidentiary observation, admitted only if it reproduces no long run of case text (the corpus C10 guard binds every tracked text) and discloses nothing withheld; otherwise it is preserved outside the repository, on the `W7-D6-HDR` precedent.
26. **Immutability:** before D3-C publication, the reviewer alone may revise a token, by explicit act. After publication, a disposition is fixed. **After reveal, no disposition may be amended by any route.**

## 6. Reveal and custody (for ADR-0057 to fix)

## 7. Calibration comparison and counts (for ADR-0057 to fix)

30. For each case of the session corpus, the comparison records exactly: the case identity, the human token from the published register, the authored intended token from the revealed record, and one relation from a closed two-member set — `agreement` · `divergence`. **That is the whole of D3's comparison.**
31. A divergence is recorded, never explained away, never "corrected", never attributed to the reviewer. Diagnosis of a divergence — the case's construction, the instrument's design, the doctrine — belongs to a later governed act (W8-D4 or its own correction), not to D3.
32. **D3-D may publish exactly two kinds of count:** per-token counts of the human dispositions, and per-relation counts of `agreement` and `divergence`. **Each count carries, in the same place, the ADR-0054 decision-31 statement that it is a count of individual human acts and nothing more.** No percentage, rate, score, grade, ranking, threshold, pass/fail or readiness interpretation — and the runway's own warning carries: a column of identical marks would itself be a finding, never a success.
