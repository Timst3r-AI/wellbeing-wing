## 3. First-contact doctrine — what DR-W7-02 must decide

**The definition must be by provenance, not by vendor, medium, or transport.** DR-W7-02 must fix three terms so that no path evades the gate by routing and no lawful path is barred by confusion:

- **Model contact** — the act of transmitting any probe, prompt, fixture, or governed material to a model and receiving text back, **local or hosted, direct or adapted, interactive or batched.** This is the crossing.
- **Generated output** — text produced by a model. The artefact class the runway's §6 nine properties govern.
- **Authored synthetic specimen** — text written by a human in this repository, deliberately carrying the properties of generated prose, for the purpose of exercising the governed handling. **A specimen is not generated output and its authoring is not model contact.**

**The consequence must be stated in the record, because it is the phase's whole thesis:** the governed-handling doctrine — record shape, paired-variant capture, no machine-selected winner, no omitted variant, human routing, the non-authority ceiling, the public-safety posture — **binds on specimens exactly as it binds on generated output.** The pattern is proven by the handling, not by the provenance of the text being handled. *The model is not the milestone.*

**Seven conjunctive preconditions for any model contact in this repository.** Five are ADR-0034 A6 decision 17's, restated and made operative for W7; two are W7's own gate discipline. **All seven, together, or no contact:**

**The one-way property, and what follows from it.** First contact, once performed, cannot be un-performed; a generated sentence cannot be un-generated by deleting the file that holds it. Therefore: **the gate is checked before, never after, and there is no retroactive authorisation path.** Any generated byte appearing before its gate is a **stop-and-report event** under runway §10 — never a record to be regularised, back-dated, or accepted with an apology attached.

**Two structural rules DR-W7-02 must carry, both closing laundering paths:**

- **No-recirculation.** Generated text may be **captured** into an evaluation record. It may **never** become an input to a prompt, a fixture, a catalogue string, a doctrine record, a summary, or any other artefact. Capture is terminal.
- **A finding is an event, not an allowlist entry.** Every lawful scan exception this repository has granted was over text a human wrote — enumerable in advance, named in a record, bounded by a proof that fails if it widens. **Generated prose cannot be enumerated in advance**, so the mechanism inverts: a finding inside generated output is a **governed event with a disposition path** — the record does not land, or it lands with the finding recorded and the text bounded or excluded. **It is never silently allowlisted.** DR-W7-02 binds the principle; **W7-D2 owns the mechanism**, and must design inside this principle rather than around it.

## 4. The synthetic-only public law — what DR-W7-01 must decide

**Synthetic-only by construction, never by scrubbing.** The W2-D4 discipline, extended unweakened to the generated-output class, exactly as ADR-0034 A4 decision 10 already extends it to every evaluation artefact. **The test is constructional, not custodial: a W7 artefact that *could* carry real-person content is unlawful regardless of whether it currently does.** Scrubbing is not a defence and has never been one here.

**Three lawful input origins, closed.** Every prompt, probe, fixture, scenario, specimen, capture, record and review input in W7 traces to exactly one of: **(a)** material authored inside this repository as synthetic for this purpose; **(b)** existing repository fixtures and fixture-derived material; **(c)** governed public repository records. **A fourth origin is unlawful**, and the no-recirculation rule of §3 means generated text is never origin (a) by later re-labelling.

**The closed exclusion list**, carried from runway §4 and made law by DR-W7-01. **No public W7 record may contain:** real-person data of any kind · any identified person's room, health, movement, food, device, contemplative, or journal material · real wearable or device data · private relationship or lived-interaction material · private model transcripts · credentials, tokens, keys, secrets, private configuration, or machine paths · a real-person evaluation channel · private adoption implementation detail · or anything that would turn the public Wing into a live personal instrument.

**The external boundary, fixed verbatim.** In public records the external side is named in exactly one form and never otherwise:

> **Any real-person adoption is a separate governed authority outside this repository.**

**The non-naming law that sentence carries.** No public record may name, describe, locate, date, specify, implement, schedule, or depend upon that authority; may not enumerate its rooms, devices, participants, or practices; may not state that it exists as a project, a plan, or a phase; and may not elaborate the sentence by synonym, paraphrase, expansion, or footnote. **The sentence is the whole of what is said.** Stating it creates nothing, opens nothing, and obliges nothing — and this repository's records are complete without it ever being said again.

**Architectural ruling, carried into this brief at acceptance.** The candidate for this brief left the fixed form open as a question, because a drafting choice would have silently bound an already-published record: the landed corpus carries the sentence in two shapes — the canonical form at `W7-AR` §1 and §4, and a grammatical variant at `W7-AR` §14, *"any real-person adoption, which remains a separate governed authority outside this repository."* **The architect has ruled. DR-W7-01 implements the ruling and does not reopen it.**

> **The fixed invariant is the clause: *a separate governed authority outside this repository*.**

**Sentence-position grammar is permitted** — leading capital at sentence start, lowercase mid-sentence, and relative-clause linkage where grammar requires it. **Nothing else is:** no synonym, no paraphrase, no expansion, and no location, date, participant, practice, project name, plan name, or implementation detail. **No Tier M erratum is required for `W7-AR` §14 on this point** — that wording is a grammatical variant, not a doctrinal inconsistency, because it preserves the invariant clause unchanged. DR-W7-01 states this rule, records this basis, and makes it mechanically checkable, with the check written so that it fails if the permitted variation widens.

**Declarability as the admission condition**, carried from ADR-0043 rule 12 into the new class: **a W7 artefact that cannot declare its inputs' synthetic provenance as data cannot land.** What cannot declare itself cannot be audited, and what cannot be audited does not land — the firewall's admission condition, applied to provenance rather than to labels.

**The non-authority ceiling**, to be carried inside every W7 record that holds generated or specimen text:

> **Generated output is evaluation evidence only. It is not truth, advice, diagnosis, therapy, safety evidence, correctness evidence, clinical validity, legal conformance, production readiness, approval, or a decision about any person.**

## 5. The first-contact anti-collapse chain

To be carried in DR-W7-02, extending ADR-0033 decision 21 and ADR-0034 decision 19 without restating or weakening either. **Normative and non-collapsible:**

**named ≠ authorised · authorised ≠ scheduled · scheduled ≠ performed · specimen ≠ contact · contact ≠ capability · generated ≠ said · fluent ≠ correct · plausible ≠ true · two variants ≠ a better variant · difference ≠ defect · captured ≠ endorsed · routed ≠ reviewed · reviewed ≠ approved · model output ≠ evidence · no contact ≠ nothing proven.**

**No record, runtime, summary, surface, or closure may collapse any link into another.** The last link is load-bearing in both directions: choosing not to contact a model proves nothing about a model, **and it does not mean W7 proved nothing** — the governed handling is the claim, and the handling can be proven whole without it.

## 11. The gate statement

**W7 names model contact. W7-D1 does not perform it, authorise it, schedule it, require it, or make it inevitable.**

Naming a crossing is how this repository has always made a crossing deliberate: the crossing is described precisely enough that stepping over it can only be a separate, authorised, recorded act — and precisely enough that stepping over it *by accident* becomes structurally hard rather than merely discouraged. **This brief and its two records leave the crossing exactly where the W6 closure record left it: named, unopened, and behind ADR-0034's first-contact boundary.** ADR-0033's hosted class stays closed and its local class stays doctrinally constructable only; **nothing here opens either, and nothing here opens either by implication.** Whether public W7 ever performs model contact at all — including the runway's Option D, under which it does not — is **W7-D3's decision, taken from the sources, through a Tier F crossing record.** D1's only duty is to ensure that decision is inherited rather than discovered.
