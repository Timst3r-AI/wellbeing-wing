## Part C — The language-law rubric (D5-T24 criteria record)

8. **The rubric's spine is D5-T24:** the threat is *"Misleading UI (governance theatre)"* — labels, ages, and provenance required at every display, shown in a form that satisfies the letter while staying *"functionally hidden"* — and the control is the surface-design review standard, verbatim: **"governance information must be legible, not merely present."** At string level — the only level D3 grades — legibility means the governance information lives **in the wording itself**, load-bearing and unremovable: a string that carries its item, label, age, state, and uncertainty inline cannot have them hidden by later typography without changing the governed string. (Whether a future surface then renders that string legibly is W6-D4/W6-D5's question, against the same D5-T24 standard, at the layer where type sizes exist.)

9. **Eight criteria, classified mechanical or review-only under the DR-W4-06 decidability discipline:**
   - **R1 — Barred-wording law (mechanical).** No ADR-0038 decision 10 barred word, phrase, or stem in the string, under word-boundary matching, with exactly the two named W1-D3 temporal collocations for *passed* lawful (the W6-D2-CD bounded exception, re-proven not widened).
   - **R2 — Placeholder discipline (mechanical).** Every bracketed slot is a generic placeholder from the contracts' own register ([profile item], [authority label], [age], and kin); no real-health token, no demographic realism (W2-D4 carried).
   - **R3 — Governance-information carriage (mechanical; the D5-T24 core).** A string that presents an item-dependent state carries the item, its authority label, and its age or status **inline as slots** — legible-not-merely-present at the wording layer.
   - **R4 — State-name fidelity (mechanical).** The string names its state in the source's own terms — current, review due, stale, expired, superseded, unknown, contradict- — never a rename, per ADR-0038 decision 5's authority-by-thesaurus bar.
   - **R5 — Authority-non-increase (review-only).** The wording carries no more certainty, approval, or finality than the governed source holds (the ADR-0039 formula, applied to prose).
   - **R6 — Uncertainty carriage and most-protective posture (review-only).** Uncertain states name their uncertainty in the output; safety-relevant variants carry the inline acknowledgement the W1-D3 semantics require; expired-means-unknown is preserved.
   - **R7 — Non-authority framing (review-only).** No verdict, completion, or approval implication; resolution routed to the user where the state requires it (contradiction resolves through review, never through recency).
   - **R8 — Accompaniment compatibility (review-only).** The wording neither contradicts nor absorbs-and-weakens the mandatory accompaniments its entry links (ADR-0040 decision 10; ADR-0039 bind vi).

10. **The disposition vocabulary is closed at four values — and deliberately contains no pass, no fail, and no conform- form**, because those are barred or outcome-smuggling registers under the standing vocabulary law: **`lawful-as-worded`** (every criterion satisfied; the exact programme answer and nothing more) · **`unlawful-as-worded`** (a criterion breached; **a stop-and-report to Tara and Ari before any correction path is even designed** — no mutation authority exists in D3) · **`routed-to-review`** (a criterion whose review-only judgement is genuinely uncertain; the human decides, per the standing routing law) · **`deferred`** (a governed unresolved posture only where a source requires one). No string is ever forced through to a preferred count.

11. **Grading never touches the words.** The catalogue remains byte-clean through the whole deliverable, proven against the `W6-CAT` hash at every landing; D3 writes grading records *about* strings, never *into* them.

12. **The ceiling, binding on every grade ever issued under this rubric:** **a grade is not approval, not display permission, not renderability, not safety, not correctness, and not a claim of any kind** — `lawful-as-worded` means the string satisfies the language-law rubric, and display still requires everything W6-D4's contracts and later law demand. This sentence travels into every D3 record.

13. **Proof obligations for W6-D3-D:** the mechanical criteria (R1–R4) execute as a deterministic, reproducible script over the live catalogue with results recorded per string · the review criteria (R5–R8) are applied per string with the reasoning recorded, never batch-asserted · every entry receives exactly one disposition with its reason · the catalogue hash is proven unchanged · the named *passed* exception is re-proven exactly bounded · and the full thirty-three-row grading table publishes in the D landing's record.
